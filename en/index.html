    Three layers

    Match day, community and social

    FutbolRadar grows in these three layers. Each line describes what is on the site today.

    1 · Match day

    Goals, bookings and substitutions

    A goal, a booking or a substitution is written into the match page event list, and the live score follows the same feed. A short story or a graphic is added only when one is actually published.

    Live scores
    2 · Community

    Comments, when the store is open

    Comments are not open on every page. On a match page the box appears only when the comment store is available. Profanity and links are rejected, and a reported comment is reviewed. If the store is off, the box stays hidden.

    3 · Social

    Share and follow

    Share buttons on a page pass its link. Follow FutbolRadar on these accounts.
