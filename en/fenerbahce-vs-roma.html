Fenerbahce vs Roma: match preview

FutbolRadar editorial analysis based on the predicted formations.

Fenerbahçe's first task in Kadıköy is to sustain pressure without opening gaps in midfield after losing the ball. In the predicted 4-2-3-1, wide players moving inside could create room for the full-backs. Sending both full-backs forward together could, however, leave space for Roma's transitions.

Early crosses alone may not trouble Roma's predicted back three. Second balls, cutbacks and preventing passes into the Dybala–Soulé line are key areas to watch. The balance between Fenerbahçe's pressure and Roma's ability to play through it could shape the match.
