At Celtic Park, the Real Game Starts in the 46th Minute

FutbolRadar 05 November 2026

Celtic–Beşiktaş is tonight at 23:00 at Celtic Park: Beşiktaş, on 7 points, face a second-half test against Celtic, on 0.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

Dear futbolradar.com readers, the whistle blows tonight at 23:00 (Turkish time) at Celtic Park [1][2]. The game is in matchday 4 of the UEFA Europa League league phase; Celtic are at home, Beşiktaş the visitors [3][4]. The referee is Germany's Sascha Stegemann, with assistants Christof Günsch and Marco Achmüller [1][5]. The confirmed record is the UEFA Europa League; this is also the first Celtic–Beşiktaş meeting to be played at Celtic Park [6].

This is my reading: tonight's story will not be a favourite-versus-outsider tale. What will be written is whether Beşiktaş's unbeaten line in Europe breaks in the second half at Celtic Park.

Seven-point Beşiktaş, zero-point Celtic Park

According to UEFA's fixture list, Beşiktaş beat Marseille 4-1 and Hoffenheim 3-2 away in the league phase, and drew 2-2 at home with Crystal Palace [3][4]. According to Anadolu Agency and the BBC table, the black-and-whites are 8th with 2 wins and 1 draw from 3 games, 7 points and a 9-5 goal difference; Celtic, after three defeats against Ferencváros, Benfica and Celta, have 0 points and are 35th [1][6]. The UEFA briefing also lists Celtic without a point and Beşiktaş on seven [7].

The BBC's pre-match statistics are as follows [6]:

Celtic have lost their last four European games; that is the club's worst European run since four consecutive Champions League defeats in October–December 2017.

Beşiktaş are unbeaten in three Europa League games (2W 1D); their longest unbeaten run in this competition since 2017.

First meeting; Beşiktaş's first competitive game against Scottish opposition, and Celtic's third against Turkish opposition (the previous two were draws with Fenerbahçe in 2015-16).

Junior Olaitan, with 3 assists in the Europa League this season, is among the competition's joint leaders.

This is my reading: Beşiktaş are higher in the table, but at Celtic Park the hosts are hungry. A seven-point advantage is not a shield on its own for an away team that cannot protect a lead in the second half.

Away fragility travels to Celtic Park

Our away record column published yesterday separated the fortress at Tüpraş from the points melting away on the road; this column takes that thesis to Celtic Park. According to the BBC, since the 2-2 at Kasımpaşa Beşiktaş have won only two of seven away games in all competitions [6]. The second half against Palace, which went from 2-0 to 2-2 on the same night, was the European face of that away fragility [4][6].

The 3-2 win away to Hoffenheim also showed that a second half in Europe can be turned around (our Hoffenheim column). But Palace and Kasımpaşa wrote the bill for second halves that could not carry a lead. Martin O'Neill told the BBC that Celtic still had a chance of going through but that the games were running out; Vincenzo Italiano said: "We may see an angry Celtic with zero points" [6].

This is my reading: at Celtic Park the real game starts in the 46th minute. If Beşiktaş go ahead, the question will not be "how many more goals" but "how calm can they stay in the second half".

Squad: Ümit suspended, Orkun a late call, Oh returns

The BBC writes that Ümit Akdağ, sent off for his challenge on Jørgen Strand Larsen in the Palace game, is suspended [6]. His name is not in the club's travelling party to Scotland either [2]. Fabio Miretti, according to Fotospor, is out for about another month [8].

The Orkun Kökçü picture changed within 48 hours. On 3 November Fotospor quoted Italiano as saying "he is not one hundred per cent, we don't want an unnecessary risk" and wrote that he would most likely not play at Celtic [8]. On 4 November, however, the club included Orkun in the 24-man travelling squad [2]; Fotospor also reported that "the captain, having recovered from injury, was given a place in the provisional squad" [9]. According to Anadolu Agency, the final decision depends on the last assessment by the medical team and the coaching staff [1]. No probable XI is invented in this column; the official starting XI will be covered separately once published.

At the other end of the story is Oh Hyeon-gyu. According to the BBC, the former Celtic forward said on his return to Parkhead: "I have to show them something"; O'Neill also said Oh might have "a point to prove" [6]. Oh has contributed two goals and an assist in three Europa League games this season [6].

This is my reading: what Beşiktaş lack at Celtic Park is tempo control more than a centre-back. If Orkun plays, the language of the midfield changes; if not, the Ndidi–Salih–Olaitan line will have to carry the second half.

Closing

Match: Celtic – Beşiktaş · UEFA Europa League league phase matchday 4 · 5 November 2026 · 23:00 Turkish time · Celtic Park · referee Sascha Stegemann [1][3][5]. Beşiktaş 7 points / 8th, Celtic 0 points / 35th [1][6]. For the team's league and European tables see Beşiktaş standings, the Beşiktaş team page, Beşiktaş form and Europa League standings; for yesterday's framing, read the away record column.

This is my reading: the test for seven-point Beşiktaş at Celtic Park is not producing goals but not losing the second half. If they manage that, they move above 8th place; if not, the zero-point hosts will look for their first three European points here.
