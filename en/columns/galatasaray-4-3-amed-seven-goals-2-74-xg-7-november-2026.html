Galatasaray 4-3 Amed: Seven Goals, 2.74 Expected Goals, and Amed Still Ahead on Goal Difference

FutbolRadar 07 November 2026

Points 28-23, goal difference with Amed: the expected-goals total of the 4-3 was 2.74.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

7 November 2026, 19:00, RAMS Park, referee Halil Umut Meler. In matchweek 11 of the Trendyol Süper Lig, Galatasaray beat Amed Sportif Faaliyetler 4-3. BBC, CNN Türk, Habertürk, T24 and FotMob all mark the result as full time. Galatasaray moved up to 28 points, Amed stayed on 23.[1][2][3][4][5]

Galatasaray's goals: 7' Rafael Leão, assisted by Lucas Torreira; 45' an Alban Lafont own goal; 59' Aleksey Batrakov, assisted by Roland Sallai; 80' Barış Alper Yılmaz, assisted by Gabriel Sara. Amed's goals: 62' Dia Saba; 65' Gift Orban, assisted by Furkan Soyalp; 90+2' Yira Sor. No source credits an assist for Sor. FotMob records the same player as Collins Sor. The sources do not describe the 45th-minute goal or the type of finish in the 62nd minute in the same way; I am leaving both as they are.[1][3][4][5]

From 3-0 to 3-2 in six minutes

The first half ended 2-0. Amed started the second half with Yira Sor and Ali Turap Bülbül in place of Mbaye Diagne and Gökhan Gül. Rafael Leão came to the touchline injured in the 47th minute and was replaced by Deniz Gül in the 50th. The scorer of the opening goal did not see out the game. Rayan Raveloson was booked; T24 has the minute as 49, BBC and FotMob as 50.[1][3][4][5]

In the 59th minute Batrakov made it 3-0 from Sallai's pass. T24 reports that in the scramble beforehand the ball had not been heading for goal, and that the goal came from a soft shot and a Lafont error. In the 61st Gift Orban was booked. In the 62nd Dia Saba made it 3-1. T24 describes it as a free kick; BBC and FotMob do not attach an assist to this goal. In the 65th Orban headed in Soyalp's assist to make it 3-2. 3-0 lasted only until the 62nd minute, in other words three minutes. In the six minutes from the 59th to the 65th, Amed scored twice.[1][4][5]

Buruk's double substitution was Leroy Sané for Yunus Akgün and Gabriel Sara for Lucas Torreira. CNN Türk and T24 time it at 68, BBC and FotMob at 69. David Bates was booked in the 74th. Furkan Soyalp went off and Rayan Lutin came on; T24 says 78, BBC and FotMob 77. In the 80th Barış Alper Yılmaz headed in for 4-2. The assist came from substitute Gabriel Sara. T24 reports that Sara's shot hit the post in the 82nd. In the 83rd Aleksey Batrakov and Eren Elmalı went off, and Renato Nhaga and Wilfried Singo came on. At 90+2 Sor made it 4-3. Cem Üstündağ came on for Raveloson in stoppage time; FotMob says 90+3, BBC 92. There is no penalty in the event lists of BBC, FotMob, T24 or CNN Türk. All three yellow cards went to Amed: Raveloson, Orban, Bates. Galatasaray had no bookings.[1][2][4][5]

This is my reading: the measure of this game is not the 4-3 itself but the six minutes between the 59th and the 65th. Amed scored twice in that window. 3-0 lived for three minutes; Orban's header wiped out the 3-1.

The 45th minute went into two different ledgers

For the goal that made it 2-0, BBC and FotMob record it as an Alban Lafont own goal, with no assist. CNN Türk's and Habertürk's result lines also call it a Lafont own goal. T24 credits the same goal to Batrakov in both its summary and its minute-by-minute: a goal from Aleksey Batrakov's shot, with goalkeeper Lafont making a big mistake. Habertürk's minute-by-minute combines the two. From Yunus Akgün's pass, Batrakov struck from a tight angle on the right of the box; Lafont tried to palm it away and the ball went in.[1][2][3][4][5]

This is my reading: calling it Batrakov's goal and calling it a Lafont own goal is entering the same contact in two ledgers. The last hand on the ball was the goalkeeper's, and the shot came from a tight angle. This goal is not Saba's free kick, nor Orban's header.

Shots 17-14, expected goals 1.47-1.27

Both teams started in a 4-2-3-1. With Abdülkerim Bardakcı suspended and Victor Osimhen out of the squad, Okan Buruk used Ismail Jakobs at centre-back and Barış Alper Yılmaz up front. Habertürk reports that Osimhen had arthroscopic surgery on his left knee today. In its pre-match squad note T24 said that for Amed, Samuel Ballet would miss out through suspension. FotMob also lists Ballet, Bardakcı and Osimhen's knee injury among the absentees for this game. CNN Türk had reported that Bardakcı's two-match ban, handed down after the derby, would be served with this game; he was not in the starting XI.[2][3][5][6]

BBC's closing statistics, Galatasaray first, then Amed:[1]

Possession 56.4% to 43.6%. FotMob rounds the same split to 56% to 44%.[5]

Shots 17 to 14.[1][5]

Shots on target 6 to 5.[1]

Corners 3 to 6.[1]

Expected goals 1.47 to 1.27. FotMob's two figures add up to 2.74.[5]

Touches in the opposition box 35 to 23.[5]

Offsides 3 to 8. Clearances 30 to 16. Yellow cards 0 to 3.[1]

FotMob named Rafael Leão, who went off in the 50th minute, player of the match with a rating of 8.3.[5]

This is my reading: seven goals sit on top of 2.74 expected goals. This was not a shower of shots. Amed had less of the ball, took more corners and hit the target five times to six. What separated the points was not the number of shots but still being a goal ahead after the 80th minute.

28 points for the leaders, goal difference with Amed at 28-15

In BBC's table updated on the evening of 7 November, Galatasaray have 9 wins, 1 draw and 1 defeat from 11 games, 27-15, and 28 points. Amed have 7 wins, 2 draws and 2 defeats from 11 games, 28-15, and 23 points. Amed have scored one more goal, goals conceded are level, and goal difference is +13 to +12 in Amed's favour. Trabzonspor are on 22 points from 10 games; they have not yet played this week's game.[1]

CNN Türk's pre-match note reported that this was the first meeting in the Süper Lig and that Amed would be the 78th different team Galatasaray have faced in their league history. The same note said the unbeaten home run had reached 40 games (32 wins, 8 draws) and that avoiding defeat would equal the club record of 41 games. The game ended in a win. The previous home defeat before the run was against Fenerbahçe on 19 May 2024.[2]

Habertürk reports that after the international break Galatasaray host Samsunspor and Amed host Çaykur Rizespor. The two teams went into the game on 25 and 23 points; by the evening they were on 28 and 23.[2][3]

This is my reading: the five-point gap is there in the table. So is the fact that goal difference is still with Amed. With Trabzonspor still to play, reading this evening as a title already settled would make the 4-3 only the leaders' night.

The absences were inside the score. Osimhen was in surgery, Bardakcı was suspended, and Leão, who scored in the 7th minute, went off in the 50th. The header that made it 4-2 came from the assist of substitute Sara. The last word, at 90+2, was Amed's. The table is on our standings page. The teams are on the Galatasaray and Amed Sportif Faaliyetler pages. The state of the league we asked about after matchweek 10 stands this evening as three points, seven goals and a goal-difference order that has not moved.
