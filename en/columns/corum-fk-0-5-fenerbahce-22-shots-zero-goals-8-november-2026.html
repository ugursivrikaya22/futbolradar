Çorum FK 0-5 Fenerbahçe: 22 Shots, Zero Goals

FutbolRadar 08 November 2026

The scoreboard said 5-0. The shot count said the opposite: 22 to 10, expected goals 2.00 to 1.30.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

Fenerbahçe beat Çorum FK 5-0 at the Çorum City Stadium at 16:00 on Sunday 8 November.[1][2][3] It was 0-2 at half-time.[1][5] The referee was Abdullah Buğra Taşkınsoy.[5][9] The full line-ups are on the match page.

The goals came from Mason Greenwood in the 12th and 63rd minutes, İrfan Can Kahveci in the 21st and 56th, and Oğuz Aydın in the 74th.[3][4][5] The assists, in order, came from Oğuz Aydın, Romelu Lukaku, Matteo Guendouzi, Levent Mercan and Kerem Aktürkoğlu.[4][5]

Five goals on the scoreboard. The shot count says the opposite: Çorum 22 shots, Fenerbahçe 10. BBC has shots on target at 8-6. NTV reports that 6 of Fenerbahçe's 10 shots were on target and 5 went in. FotMob measures expected goals at 2.00 to 1.30 and possession at 39-61 per cent. BBC has possession at 39.2-60.8 per cent. Ederson made 9 saves, the Çorum goalkeeper 2.[4][5][6]

Our preview column had read this evening as a one-goal away win for Fenerbahçe. The score beat that thesis by four goals. The shot map did not.

Six on target, five goals

In the 3rd minute Oğuz Aydın's shot from outside the box went wide. In the 9th minute Jesús Ramírez found himself inside the six-yard box from Cengiz Ünder's pass; Ederson saved. NTV calls that save, which kept it 0-0, the turning point.[1][6]

In the 12th minute Greenwood, picking up Oğuz's long pass on the right, found the net: 0-1. In the 21st İrfan Can, shooting from outside the box after a pass from Lukaku, doubled the lead. Half-time 0-2.[1][4][5]

In the 38th minute Mohamed Diomandé was booked. In the 54th Ederson kept out Ramírez's header from Ylber Ramadani's cross; in the same minute Nélson Semedo was booked. In the 56th İrfan Can beat his man on the edge of the box from Guendouzi's pass and scored his second: 0-3. In the 57th N'Golo Kanté came on for İrfan Can and Vedat Muriqi for Lukaku. In the 63rd Greenwood made it 0-4 from Levent Mercan's pass. In the 64th Kerem Aktürkoğlu replaced Greenwood and Mert Müldür replaced Semedo. In the 74th Oğuz Aydın added the fifth from Kerem's pass. In the 77th Mert Hakan Yandaş came on for Guendouzi.[4][5]

For Çorum, Ermin Mahmić made way for Youssoufa Moukoko in the 58th minute and Alexandre Penetra for Arda Şengül in the 65th. Cengiz Ünder went off in the 61st.[4][5]

Fenerbahçe took their league goal tally to 29. BBC's table also shows 29 scored and 10 conceded. Fotomaç calls them the league's highest scorers on that basis; it reports that Ederson kept his 6th clean sheet and that 5-0 is the biggest away win of the season. Away from home they have scored 12 and conceded 4. İrfan Can scored twice in his 200th game. Fotomaç counts his league goals at 5 and assists at 3. Greenwood reached his 10th goal in all competitions; Fotomaç notes that in all three league games in which he has scored (Kayserispor, Eyüpspor, Çorum FK) he has scored twice. The club presented the shirt bearing 200 through director of football Oğuz Çetin and Belözoğlu.[2][5][7]

2.00 expected goals, zero goals

BBC has corners at 7-1 in Çorum's favour, and passes at 382-617 and pass accuracy at 86.1-90.3 per cent in Fenerbahçe's favour. FotMob counts touches in the opposition box at 33-19 in Çorum's favour.[4][5]

Burak Yılmaz put the difference down to finishing: "The difference between Fenerbahçe and us is that they score the chances they create, and unfortunately we couldn't score ours today. If we had, it would have been different." He added that he does not treat the statistics as an excuse. AA reports that he was happy with the first-half performance and that he said: "We can't make contact, we can't be physical, we can't be aggressive." "We had worked very well, but it was a disappointing night for us. I apologise to all our supporters and to our president."[1]

Ederson told the night differently: "My performance was not at the level I wanted, so this was not very easy for me. But I always stayed calm." He said his only aim was to help the team.[6]

Emre Belözoğlu flagged the crosses as a minus and said Ederson's saves had kept the team in the game. The line in Fanatik arrives at the same place: "We allowed some crosses, that's a minus." He added that the organisation needs to improve both defensively and offensively.[1][8]

This is my reading: 5-0 is a scoreline of superiority. It was not a match of superiority. Çorum turned 8 shots on target into 0 goals, Fenerbahçe turned 6 into 5. 2.00 expected goals came to nothing; 1.30 expected goals became five. Burak's sentence is the shot count itself.

Kanté started on the bench

Both teams lined up 4-2-3-1. Çorum: Marcos Felipe; Alexandre Penetra, Çağlar Söyüncü, Hrvoje Smolčić, Andrei Borza; Ylber Ramadani, Ermin Mahmić; Cengiz Ünder, Mohamed Diomandé, Alexandros Kyziridis; Jesús Ramírez. Fenerbahçe: Ederson; Nélson Semedo, Milan Škriniar, Oppong, Levent Mercan; İsmail Yüksek, Matteo Guendouzi; Mason Greenwood, İrfan Can Kahveci, Oğuz Aydın; Romelu Lukaku. BBC lists the captains as Söyüncü and Škriniar.[3][4][5]

Sporx reports two changes from the XI in the 3-2 win over Liverpool. The suspended Archie Brown was out and N'Golo Kanté was on the bench; Levent Mercan and Oğuz Aydın came in. The injured Marco Asensio and Nathan Aké, who has not returned to team training, were also missing.[9]

The plan Fanatik reported on Sunday morning was different. Kanté, Guendouzi and İsmail Yüksek in midfield; Vedat Muriqi instead of Lukaku up front. That XI did not take the field. Muriqi and Kanté came on in the 57th minute, with the score at 0-3. All five goals were scored by the starting XI.[4][10]

Belözoğlu is 46. Sporx and Fotomaç put the gap at around five and a half years; this away game was the first competitive match of his second spell. "A perfect start for us," he said. He thought one 20-25 minute period of the game was very good, and added that preparation had lasted two days. In the same press conference he said "There are things that need fixing" and "The football can develop a lot more." Asked about the 9-point gap to the leaders, he said they need to take it game by game and that he had told the dressing room "there are 72 points left".[1][7][8][9]

On 5 November Burak Yılmaz had framed this game as a threshold. Beating Fenerbahçe would be the same as the Erzurum win in points, but something else in terms of confidence. AA records his first four league games in charge as 3 wins and 1 defeat. The only home game among those four was a 2-1 against Rizespor. This evening's home game ended 0-5. He said he had picked the squad himself and the responsibility was his.[1][11][12]

This is my reading: the score in his first competitive game was 5-0, and the coach's own yardstick was the first 20-25 minutes. Fanatik's Kanté-Muriqi plan stayed on the bench. Oğuz Aydın assisted in the 12th minute and scored in the 74th. Levent Mercan assisted in the 63rd. Kanté and Muriqi did not score.

Nine goals' difference, nineteen goals' difference

At the end of matchweek 10 on 2 November, Fenerbahçe were 6th: 10 games, 24 goals scored, 10 conceded, goal difference +14, 16 points. Çorum FK were 7th: 21 scored, 16 conceded, goal difference +5, 16 points. The gap in goal difference was 9 goals.[13]

This evening Fenerbahçe moved up to 19 points.[2][3][7] Çorum stayed on 16.[3][5][7] In BBC's table at 15:47 UTC on 8 November, Fenerbahçe are 5th, 29-10, goal difference +19; Çorum are 9th, 21-21, goal difference 0. In the same table Beşiktaş are 6th on 19 points from 10 games and shown as not yet having played. Kocaelispor are on 19 points from 11 games. Galatasaray have 28.[5] AA reports that Belözoğlu was asked about the 9-point gap to the leaders; the difference between 28 and 19 is 9.[1][5]

The table is on our Fenerbahçe standings, Çorum FK standings and Süper Lig standings pages.

Claim: on 4 November T24 reported that Volkan Demirel could be brought in to replace director of football Oğuz Çetin. The club's 8 November match report still says "our Director of Football Oğuz Çetin"; it was he who presented the 200th-game shirt alongside Belözoğlu. The claim has not been confirmed.[2][14]

This is my reading: the preview's thesis held in direction, but not in margin. A one-goal away win would not have pulled Çorum's +5 goal difference to 21-21. The 0-5 stretched a nine-goal gap in goal difference to nineteen. The shots stayed with Çorum. Fenerbahçe scored what they created. Belözoğlu called the start perfect and, in the same breath, listed the crosses, the organisation and Ederson.
