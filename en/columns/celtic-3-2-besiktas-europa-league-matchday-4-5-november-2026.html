Celtic 3-2 Beşiktaş: The Side That Kept the Ball Could Not Keep the Score

FutbolRadar 06 November 2026

The ball was with Beşiktaş, the tempo with Celtic: the second-half test told by a 3-2.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

The whistle went at Celtic Park and the picture flipped: in matchday four of the UEFA Europa League league phase, Celtic beat Beşiktaş 3-2.[1][2][3] It was 0-0 at half-time, and all five goals came in the second half.[1][2] The referee was Germany's Sascha Stegemann.[2][3] Beşiktaş suffered their first defeat of the league phase and stayed 8th on 7 points. Celtic moved up to 32nd on 3 points.[1][3]

This is my reading: this was not a game the favourites won. The side that kept the ball, got into the box more often and went ahead from a penalty was Beşiktaş. The side that wrote the score was Celtic, who changed their tempo with a triple substitution in the 61st minute.

The chaos started on 54 and ended on 87

54' Junior Olaïtan (Beşiktaş): Johnstone saved the penalty, Olaïtan tucked in the rebound (0-1).

62' Callum Osmand (Celtic), assisted by Callum McGregor (1-1).

78' Camilo Durán (Celtic), assisted by Osmand (2-1).

86' Orkun Kökçü (Beşiktaş), assisted by Ernest Poku, from distance (2-2). BBC gives the minute of this goal as 85.[1][2]

87' Camilo Durán (Celtic): finished the ball that dropped to him from Tierney's shot (3-2).

The penalty was awarded in the 52nd minute for Lotomba's challenge on Poku.[1][2] Around the 61st minute Celtic brought on Osmand, Durán and Donovan together.[1] For Beşiktaş, Agbadou and Oh went off in the 70th minute and Djaló and Vlahović came on. In the 76th minute Kökçü replaced Salih.[1][2][3]

This is my reading: settling for an equaliser eight minutes after going ahead is not managing the score; it is failing to carry it.

The preview thesis held: the real game started on 46

In our preview column we asked the question plainly: if Beşiktaş go ahead, how calm can they stay in the second half? The first half ended 0-0. According to BBC, Celtic had only two touches in the Beşiktaş box in the first half. All five goals that followed came in the second half.[1][2] Celtic's equaliser came 43 seconds after Osmand came on.[1] According to BBC's season summary, Beşiktaş have dropped 5 points this season from winning positions.[1]

This is my reading: the second-half test was failed. Beşiktaş controlled the ball and the space, but could not control the game's breaking-point minutes.

61.5% possession, 32 touches in the box to 13, and still 3-2

BBC's match statistics (Celtic first, then Beşiktaş):[1]

Possession 38.5% to 61.5%.

Shots 10 to 15, shots on target 6 to 4.

Touches in the opposition box 13 to 32.

Goalkeeper saves 2 to 3, corners 5 to 7.

Passes 373 to 599, pass accuracy 75.3% to 85.6%.

Yellow cards: Nygren (60') for Celtic, Murillo (90+4) and Topçu (90+5) for Beşiktaş.

The permitted sources do not give an xG figure for this game, so I have not included one.

This is my reading: these numbers refute the claim that "Beşiktaş produced nothing", but they also clearly show that the superiority they built did not turn into goals.

The substitutes turned the game, Beşiktaş are 8th

According to BBC's analysis, O'Neill's triple substitution turned the game. Osmand and Durán produced three goals, and Osmand added an assist.[1] Kökçü returned from injury and scored, but the 87th-minute goal left that strike short.[1][2][3]

According to BBC's table, Beşiktaş have 2 wins, 1 draw and 1 defeat from 4 games. Goals 11-8, 7 points, 8th place. Celtic are 32nd on 3 points.[1] The top 8 go straight into the last 16, and the teams from 9th to 24th play in the play-offs.[1]

For the team's current situation, see the Beşiktaş team page, Beşiktaş form and Europa League standings pages.

This is my reading: to hold on to 8th place in Europe, they first need to learn to hold on to a lead taken in the second half.
