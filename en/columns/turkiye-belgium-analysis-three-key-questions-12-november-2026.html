A Striker, De Bruyne, the Last 15 Minutes: Three Questions That Will Decide Türkiye vs Belgium

FutbolRadar 10 November 2026

France 10, Italy 7, Belgium 6, Türkiye 0: without a win in İzmir, last place in the group is confirmed.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

The table: only one way out of last place

The arithmetic of Thursday night's game in İzmir is simple but harsh. After four matchdays of UEFA Nations League A, Group 1, France have 10 points, Italy 7 and Belgium 6. Türkiye are bottom without a point. We covered the emotional story of the game in an earlier piece; here we put Belgium and Sergen Yalçın's ideas on the table. [1]

Two games remain: Belgium in İzmir on 12 November and France in Bordeaux on 15 November. Belgium already have 6 points; without a win in this game, finishing bottom of the group is confirmed. This season the bottom team in a group is not relegated automatically: of the four groups' fourth-placed teams, the worst two drop to League B and the best two go into a play-off. So every point counts, but the only way out of last place is to win. [9] [5] [2]

Sergen Yalçın spoke openly about the risk of relegation when he named his squad. That is why the game in İzmir is far more than a first match. [5]

Four games, two goals scored, eleven conceded

The numbers show that the problem runs both ways. At home we lost 1-0 to France and 4-1 to Italy; away we lost 3-0 to Belgium and 3-1 to Italy. Four games, only 2 goals scored, 11 conceded. [3]

A team that scores half a goal a game and concedes almost three does not have just one problem. The defence breaks too easily, and the attack finds nobody in the box. That is why the Montella era was criticised above all for playing without a striker. [5]

Sergen Yalçın's first job starts right there: first shut the door, then find a way to goal. He has to do both at once, and with only a handful of training sessions.

Belgium: strong but fragile

Mark van Bommel has been in charge of Belgium since July. In the Nations League they beat Italy 2-0 away and us 3-0, but lost twice to France. They have scored 6 and conceded 5 in four games. [4]

There are plenty of familiar faces in the squad. De Bruyne, who scored twice against us in October, is here again. Doku missed the first four games and is now in the squad. What is interesting is that two Süper Lig stars will be facing us: Fenerbahçe's striker Lukaku, and Trossard, who made three assists for Beşiktaş last weekend. Both know our league, our stadiums and our players very well. [4] [8] [10]

But Belgium have a weakness too. Away in France they led 1-0 and lost 4-1 after conceding four goals in the last 15 minutes. There are absences as well: Debast is injured, De Winter was not called up, and Openda and Fofana are not on the list. A Türkiye side that stays on its feet for ninety minutes could make life difficult for this team. [1] [4]

Sergen Yalçın's idea: play with a striker

We do not know the starting eleven yet; the coach has not named anyone, saying only that he will start with players who are physically ready. So I will not invent a predicted line-up here. But he has said very clearly what he wants: "We will play with a striker; our system will be different." [6] [5]

His stance on Arda Güler is clear too: "Whatever position he wants to play, we will play him there." In midfield, alongside Arda, are Hakan Çalhanoğlu and Orkun Kökçü; up front, Barış Alper Yılmaz and Enes Ünal. In goal four names are competing: Uğurcan Çakır, Berke Özer, Ersin Destanoğlu and first-time call-up Serhat Öztaşdelen. [6] [5]

The selection criterion is clear as well: "Whoever deserves it will be in this team." The camp began in İstanbul, and Aral Şimşir's treatment is ongoing. A short preparation, the idea of a big change. [6] [7]

Three questions that will decide the game

1. Who will lead the line? The striker was the coach's very first word. Enes Ünal is a classic number nine, while Barış Alper can play both wide and up front and runs in behind with his pace. For a team that has scored two goals in four games, who stands in the box will be the game's first answer.

2. Who will shut down De Bruyne? Two of the goals in October's 3-0 came from his boot. Hakan and Orkun not giving him room to breathe in midfield means cutting off the balls to Lukaku at the source. In this game, midfield discipline matters as much as Arda's creativity. [8]

3. Who will still be standing in the last 15 minutes? Belgium fell apart late on in France. The coach's emphasis on physical readiness points exactly here. If the game stays tight, with the backing of the İzmir crowd, the final minutes could turn in our favour. [1]

What I expect

It would be unfair to expect beautiful football from this game. A new coach, a short camp, the heavy weight of four defeats. What I expect is much more basic: a team that does not concede easy goals, finds someone in the box and does not fall apart over 90 minutes.

Belgium are ahead of us in quality; there is no need to hide that. But the France game showed this team can come undone under pressure. A Türkiye that starts with discipline and stays patient can take its first points in İzmir.

The arithmetic is plain: if we do not win, last place in the group is confirmed. If we win, hope lives on and the Sergen Yalçın era begins the way it should, with a win. On Thursday night there is only one option.
