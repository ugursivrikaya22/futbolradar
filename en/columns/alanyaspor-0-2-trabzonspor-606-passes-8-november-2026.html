606 Passes, One Post and a Six-Match Vow in Alanya: Alanyaspor 0-2 Trabzonspor

futbolradar 08 November 2026

Fırtına is second on 25 points, three behind leaders Galatasaray.

Share this column
fShare on Facebook XShare on X

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

On Thursday night we lost 1-0 to Freiburg, got up on Friday morning, and by Sunday evening we were in Alanya. We asked whether the defeat from three days earlier would still sit in this side's head. The answer came in the 34th minute. Trabzonspor beat Alanyaspor 2-0 in matchweek 11 of the Süper Lig. The goals came from Wagner Pina in the 17th minute and Mohamed Salah in the 34th [1][2]. That score means a sixth straight league win. Fırtına is second on 25 points, three behind leaders Galatasaray [2][3]. This piece will not only recount the score. It will describe a side that finished the match in the first half, then held its breath in the second half while looking at the post.

The opponent we could not beat across the previous three meetings went quiet with two goals

Our memory is good. There was no win across the previous three meetings with this opponent: 1-1 on 8 November 2025, a 0-1 cup defeat on 17 December 2025, and 1-1 again on 11 April 2026 [2]. A full year later, on the same date, that run ended.

The opening goal came from a centre-back's eye. Pina, running in behind the defence onto Nwaiwu's long ball, sent the ball into the net with his right foot from the right side of the penalty area [1][3]. For the second, Muçi's through ball bounced off Onuachu. Salah found the ball in front of him, got away from his marker and put it into the net with his left foot [1]. BBC's match record gives the assist to Onuachu [2].

This take: Both goals share one thing. Each was born from a straight run in behind the defence. Alanyaspor started the match with three centre-backs [2]. Fırtına found the space behind those three centre-backs twice inside the opening half-hour. I cannot write that off as luck. It was a plan.

606 passes: loving the ball, tiring the opponent

The numbers confirm what the stand saw. Trabzonspor kept the ball for 61.6% of the match, played 606 passes, and 89.4% of those passes found their man. Alanyaspor stayed on 363 passes [2]. We led the shot count 21 to 14, and shots on target 5 to 2 [2]. The home side had to clear the ball 26 times [2]. That shows they spent most of the match breathing in front of their own penalty area.

This take: 600 passes away from home show a side that trusts itself. I would have expected a side that lost on Thursday to try to hide the ball on Sunday and shrink the pitch. Reis's side did the opposite and wanted the ball. Losing in Europe and refusing to give the ball away in the league tells me this squad's character more clearly than any goal.

But the second half: the post was in our stand too

Let us be honest. The match looked over by the 34th minute, but it was not. At 58, Salah's left-footed strike from outside the box drifted just wide. At 74, from Cabral's cross, Onuachu sent the ball out at the back post [1][3]. The longer we failed to score, the more Alanya hoped. At 85, Onana saved Omar Ben Ali's shot inside the six-yard box. At 90+2, another ball Ben Ali touched came back off the post [1][3]. Of 21 shots, 5 hit the target, and we were caught offside 4 times [2].

This take: A warning sits inside the sixth win. A side that cannot score a third goal while 2-0 up spends the closing ten minutes biting its nails. Tonight Onana's glove and one post saved us. The leaders are three points ahead of us. In a race like this we cannot trust the post to stay on our side every week. We have to learn to play the second half the way we played the opening half.

Six from six with Reis: a run, or an identity?

Thomas Reis has taken charge of six league matches and won all six. Galatasaray, Samsunspor, Beşiktaş, Çaykur Rizespor, Gaziantep FK and now Alanyaspor [3]. The side has conceded only 8 goals across 11 weeks and is second with a +18 goal difference [2]. The next match, after the international break, is at home against Eyüpspor. The opponent is bottom on 3 points [2][3].

This take: What separates a run from an identity is what you do after a bad week. Coming back from Alanya with three points on top of the Freiburg defeat looks like identity to me. Even so, the stand will give the real test. It falls to us to wait through the international break without letting this side go cold, and to set our home alight from the opening minute against Eyüp.

Nobody in Alanya watched unforgettable football tonight. But 606 passes, two runs in behind and one post showed this side's stubbornness. Three points separate us from the leaders. Fırtına believes, and we are right behind them.

— futbolradar
