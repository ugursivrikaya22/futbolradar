The Crescent and Star Above All: Belgium Is Sergen Yalçın's First Test

FutbolRadar 10 November 2026

Thursday 12 November, 20:00 Turkish time · İzmir, Gürsel Aksel Stadium · UEFA Nations League A, Group 1, matchday 5

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

A new page in İzmir

At 20:00 on Thursday evening a whistle will blow at the Gürsel Aksel Stadium in İzmir, and a new era will begin for the Turkish national team. The opponents on matchday five of UEFA Nations League A, Group 1 are Belgium. On the bench: Sergen Yalçın. [1]

This is Yalçın's first competitive match in charge of the crescent and star. It is not an easy first game: in front of us is a Belgium side with De Bruyne and Lukaku, and behind us a heavy summer and an even heavier autumn.

But sometimes the hardest games make the best beginnings. Especially when the opponent is Belgium, a very special night comes alive in this nation's memory.

A return awaited for 24 years, a dream over in three games

In the summer this country played at a World Cup again after 24 years. We got past Romania and Kosovo in the play-offs, and millions sat in front of their screens. The children of 2002 brought their own children along and waited to live the same excitement once more. [3]

It did not happen. We lost 2-0 to Australia and 1-0 to Paraguay. We beat the USA 3-2 in the last game, but three points were not enough to get out of the group in the first tournament played with 48 teams. The dream ended where it began. [2]

The autumn was harder still. In the Nations League we lost 1-0 to France, 4-1 at home and 3-1 away to Italy, and 3-0 away to Belgium. Four games, four defeats: the first time since 1987-88 that we had lost four in a row. The Montella era, which had taken us to the quarter-finals at Euro 2024, ended in October by decision of the TFF. [4] [3]

"This is the people's national team"

On 20 October the TFF handed the reins to Sergen Yalçın. The national team's 48th head coach took the job on a four-year contract. A coach who won league and cup titles with Beşiktaş and has managed teams all over Anatolia, he did not hide his targets either: the European Championship and the World Cup. [5] [6]

One sentence from his appointment stayed with me: "The crescent and star is above everything." Naming his first provisional squad, he opened the door to everyone: "This is the people's national team." [5] [7]

The first squad carries a message too. 26 names, including Arda Güler and Hakan Çalhanoğlu. Ten players from the previous squad, Kerem Aktürkoğlu among them, were not called up. Kenan Yıldız is injured; he wanted to come but is continuing his treatment at Juventus. A new coach, a new page, new competition for places. [7]

The memory of this shirt

There is something we must not forget in difficult days: this shirt has already shown what it can do.

In 2002 Şenol Güneş's side finished third at the World Cup, beating South Korea 3-2 in Daegu to hang the bronze medal around our necks. At Euro 2008 we came from 2-0 down to beat the Czech Republic 3-2, scored in the final seconds of extra time against Croatia, went through on penalties and met Germany in the semi-final. [8] [9]

And so recently that it almost feels like yesterday, in 2024 we knocked out Austria to reach the quarter-finals, then lost 2-1 to the Netherlands in a game in which we had taken the lead. This team knows how to get up from where it falls. Because this shirt carries years of stubbornness. [3]

That night in Brussels

When we think of Belgium, the first date that comes to mind is 19 June 2000. At Euro 2000, hosts Belgium only needed a draw in Brussels. Hakan Şükür scored twice, Belgium went out of their own tournament, and Türkiye got out of a group at a major tournament for the first time in their history. [10] [9]

A small detail of that night carries big meaning today. Okan Buruk was on the pitch. And on the bench sat a young Sergen Yalçın. 26 years later, against the same opponent, he will stand at the front of the bench. [10]

The rivalry is actually balanced. The two sides have met 12 times: three wins for us, five draws and four defeats. We won 3-2 in Brussels in 2004 and 3-2 again in İstanbul in 2010. In the last meeting, in October, we lost 3-0 to two goals from De Bruyne and one from Lukaku. This time Belgium come to İzmir for the first time; there could be no better stage for revenge. [11] [12]

İzmir, we stand on Thursday night

Let us not fool anyone: the picture is grim. We have no points from four group games, and three days after Belgium comes the trip to France. One game will not fix everything. But every new era begins with a first step, and how that step is taken shapes what follows. [1]

We are not expecting a miracle from Sergen Yalçın on Thursday night. What we expect is an attitude: a team that wants the ball, is not afraid and deserves the shirt. The stands will take care of the rest; İzmir is not a city that leaves this shirt alone.

The story he watched from the bench that night in Brussels, Sergen Yalçın now begins to write in his own hand. The crescent and star above all. Come on, Türkiye!
