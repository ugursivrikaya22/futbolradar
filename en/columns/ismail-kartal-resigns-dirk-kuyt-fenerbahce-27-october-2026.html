İsmail Kartal Goes, Dirk Kuyt Arrives: An Era Ends at Fenerbahçe and a Warrior Takes the Dugout

FutbolRadar 28 October 2026

132 days, 18 games and a 23-to-4 shot count at RAMS Park: the Kartal era is over, and Kadıköy's 'Warrior' Kuyt has taken the dugout.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

Dear futbolradar.com readers, there are some evenings when a yellow-and-navy heart breaks and starts beating again at the same time.

Tuesday 27 October 2026, 20:43. A two-word headline appeared on Fenerbahçe's official website: "PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT" [1].

And the next morning, at 11:03 on 28 October, the second announcement came. There was now a "Warrior" in the dugout: Dirk Kuyt [2].

The Kartal era in summary: 132 days, 18 games

İsmail Kartal took charge of Fenerbahçe for the fourth time on 18 June [3].

The bare numbers of this spell are as follows [3][4]:

Time in charge: 132 days

Games: 18 (9 Süper Lig, 9 UEFA Champions League including qualifiers)

Won / Drawn / Lost: 9 / 5 / 4

In Europe: 4 wins, 4 draws and 1 defeat in 9 games

In the league: 5 wins, 1 draw and 3 defeats in 9 games, 16 points

Gornik Zabrze, Sturm Graz and Lyon were knocked out; Fenerbahçe reached the Champions League for the first time in 18 years [4].

This is my reading: each of those qualifying nights was a line of an epic written for the longing these fans have carried inside them for years.

But there was another side to the coin. In the league phase, draws with Roma and Slavia Prague, and a 6-0 demolition away at Aston Villa [4].

In the league, a season that began with defeat to Gençlerbirliği produced only 7 points in the first 5 weeks. At Kadıköy they lost 2-1 to Beşiktaş [4].

Then came a run of wins over Eyüpspor, Çaykur Rizespor and Alanyaspor [4]. Just as hope was turning green, RAMS Park came along.

And let us not forget: Kartal had also resigned after the Roma game on 10 September, and stayed on after President Aziz Yıldırım stepped in [3][4].

The moment of resignation and the last derby: 23 to 4

26 October 2026, Süper Lig matchweek 9, RAMS Park. Galatasaray 2-1 Fenerbahçe [5].

Victor Osimhen struck twice, in the 29th and 44th minutes. İrfan Can Kahveci pulled one back in the 62nd minute from Oğuz Aydın's pass [5][6].

According to FotMob's match report, the numbers are brutal [7]:

Possession: 62% – 38%

Shots: 23 – 4

Expected goals (xG): 2.27 – 0.31

Fenerbahçe did not manage a single shot in the first half [7].

This is my reading: these numbers described not a defeat but a surrender; the scoreboard said 2-1, but the gap on the pitch was far bigger.

This is my reading: İrfan Can's goal was the only spark on a dark night; a cry reminding us that the yellow-and-navy spirit was still there.

The next day the club's statement came [1]:

"Our Head Coach İsmail Kartal shared his decision to step down with our board, and following the talks held, this decision has been considered within a framework of mutual understanding and respect."

The club thanked Kartal "for his effort, his dedication and his commitment to Fenerbahçe" [1].

No official public statement came from Kartal himself. According to HT Spor, he told President Yıldırım [8]:

"My mind can't take it any more, and I can't motivate myself either. I also want to clear the way for the club. I believe this is what's best for both me and the club."

This is my reading: in these words there is less the voice of a coach than that of a tired Fenerbahçe man who carried this crest on his back for years. Thank you, coach İsmail.

Kuyt's Fenerbahçe past and his return

Now to that name. Dirk Kuyt.

The Dutchman joined Fenerbahçe in 2012 and stayed in Istanbul until 2015 [9].

His numbers in the yellow-and-navy shirt [9]:

Competitive games: 95

Goals: 26

His greatest memory: the 2013-14 Süper Lig title [10][11]. When Fenerbahçe sealed the championship with a 0-0 draw against Çaykur Rizespor on 27 April 2014, Kuyt was on the pitch [12].

After Fenerbahçe he returned to Feyenoord and retired in 2017 [10].

As a coach he managed ADO Den Haag, Beerschot and FC Dordrecht; in June 2026 he returned to Fenerbahçe as Kartal's assistant [10][11].

The club's announcement is clear: Kuyt is the first team's technical lead "until our new head coach is appointed" [2].

This is my reading: Kadıköy's "Warrior" is drawing his sword from the dugout this time. These stands still remember his sweat-soaked shirt.

Uncertainty and where things stand

Fenerbahçe are 5th on 16 points after 9 weeks. Leaders Galatasaray are on 22; the gap is 6 points [6][10].

Kuyt's first test is Göztepe at Kadıköy on Saturday 31 October [5]. Then, on Wednesday 4 November, Liverpool in the Champions League [10][11].

Yes, you read that right: Kuyt will host his old club Liverpool, for whom he scored 71 goals in 285 games [11].

There is no official announcement yet on who the permanent head coach will be [2].

This is my reading: football sometimes writes scripts no novelist would dare to; the Liverpool night could be the one that changes the fate of this season.

Closing

One coach has said goodbye, and an old hero has taken the helm. The table is bitter, the calendar merciless.

This is my reading: but this club knows best how to get back on its feet on nights like these. Now is not the time to sulk, but to pull together.

Kadıköy, Saturday is waiting for you.
