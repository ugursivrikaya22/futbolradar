The Süper Lig at the Break: Breathing Space at the Top, Alarm Bells for Eyüpspor

FutbolRadar 09 November 2026

Galatasaray 28, Trabzonspor 25, Amed 23, Beşiktaş 22, Kasımpaşa 20, Fenerbahçe 19.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

11 weeks, six teams, nine points

Week 11 of the Trendyol Süper Lig is done and the ball has been handed over to the national teams. Galatasaray lead on 28 points, with Trabzonspor three behind. But the real story is how crowded the top of the table is. [1]

The top six are separated by nine points: Galatasaray 28, Trabzonspor 25, Amed 23, Beşiktaş 22, Kasımpaşa 20, Fenerbahçe 19. Kocaelispor are also waiting at the door on 19 points. Full table: Süper Lig standings. [1]

So this break is a stop, not a verdict. The first third of the season has not yet allowed anyone to break away from anyone.

Three stories at the top

Galatasaray are winning, but wounded. The Lions went into the break on a five-match winning run. The breathless 4-3 win over Amed extended their unbeaten league run at RAMS Park to 41 games and equalled the club record from the Fatih Terim era. But the price is heavy: Osimhen had surgery after his injury in the Stuttgart game and is out for about six weeks, and Rafael Leão was injured against Amed and will be sidelined for four to six weeks. [2] [3] [4]

Trabzonspor are quiet but deadly. Thomas Reis's side have won all of their last six games. They have conceded only 8 goals in 11 games, the fewest in the league. Salah is right in the middle of the scoring race with nine goals. [2] [1]

Amed are making history. Playing in the Süper Lig for the first time in their history, Diyarbakır side Amed are third on 23 points. They lost 4-3 at RAMS Park, but scored three times and frightened the leaders until the final minute. Gift Orban is the league's top scorer with ten goals. [5] [2]

The crowd behind

Beşiktaş have two faces. Beşiktaş are fourth on 22 points. They have won six out of six at Dolmabahçe, and Vlahovic has reached nine goals. But away from home they have taken only 4 of 15 points; that is what separates them from the top. [7] [2]

Kasımpaşa are hard to beat. Fifth on 20 points, Kasımpaşa have lost only once in 11 games, the same number as the leaders. What holds them back from climbing higher is five draws. [1]

Fenerbahçe on a new page. Fenerbahçe are sixth on 19 points but are the league's highest scorers with 29 goals. Emre Belözoğlu, who took over from İsmail Kartal, won 5-0 away at Çorum in his first game. Four defeats, however, explain why the team is sixth. Kocaelispor sit right behind on the same points. [2] [1]

Alarm at the bottom

At the foot of the table, Eyüpspor are alone and in deep trouble. One win and 3 points from 11 games, and defeats in all of their last eight. Even more striking is the goal tally: 3 scored, 30 conceded. This team's problem is not a single game or a single player. [1] [2]

Just above them is a squeezed group. Samsunspor are 17th on 7 points, but beat Konyaspor 1-0 to go into the break with some breathing room. Gaziantep FK and Konyaspor have 8 points each; Gençlerbirliği, on 11, are close to the safe zone for now. [1]

For the teams at the bottom, the break is not a luxury but something like a last chance. Whoever does not use these two weeks well should prepare for a much colder winter.

A four-way race for the Golden Boot

The scoring race is at least as tight as the title race. Amed's striker Gift Orban leads with ten goals. Right behind him are three stars on nine each: Vlahovic, Salah and Osimhen. Shomurodov follows on eight; Benedyczak, Juan and Muriqi have seven each. [2]

The first turning point of this race came not on the pitch but in the treatment room. Osimhen's absence of about six weeks could shift the balance of the Golden Boot race as much as Galatasaray's. [4]

Vlahovic has been back on the rise under Italiano in recent weeks, while Salah is the top scorer of a Trabzonspor side on a six-match winning run. The weeks after the break will show how firmly Orban sits on the throne. [6]

First whistle after the break

The league returns on 21 November, and week 12 is critical from day one. Galatasaray host Samsunspor at RAMS Park; if they avoid defeat, they will hold the record outright with a 42-game run. On the same day Kocaelispor and Fenerbahçe, both on 19 points, face each other. [1] [3]

On Sunday Beşiktaş travel to Konya, while Trabzonspor play bottom side Eyüpspor at Papara Park. Amed host Rizespor and Kasımpaşa welcome Alanyaspor. And eight days later, on 30 November, comes the Beşiktaş–Galatasaray derby at Dolmabahçe. [1]

The break may look like a pause, but it is really a breath. Three points at the top, nine points across the top six, an alarm at the bottom. The real story of the Süper Lig will start to be written after these two weeks.
