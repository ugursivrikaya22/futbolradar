Ten Men, Three Assists, 3-0

futbolradar 08 November 2026

The score is 3-0. The night changed at the 22nd minute, with ten men.

Share this column
fShare on Facebook XShare on X

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Beşiktaş beat Gençlerbirliği 3-0 on Sunday 8 November at 21:00 at Tüpraş Stadium. It was matchweek 11 of the Trendyol Süper Lig. Atilla Karaoğlan refereed, with assistants Gökhan Barcın and Murat Ergin Gözütok and fourth official Alpaslan Şen. The fixture card is on the match page.[1][2][3]

The club page says that at the 90th minute the side left the pitch 3-0 ahead. The opening half ended 2-0.[2]

Dusan Vlahovic scored at 20 and 45. Ernest Poku scored at 85.[1][2]

The pre-match column held an unbeaten home run of five together with an 8-9 return across the previous five matches. Tonight the score was 3-0. On the beIN minute log and the club page, the visitors were down to ten from the 22nd minute. The opening sentence of the beIN report puts that red card in the 23rd minute.[1][2]

20, 22, 45

At 20, Vlahovic met Trossard's through ball, entered the penalty area and struck it along the ground. The ball went in beside İrfan Can Eğribayat: 1-0.[1]

Metehan Baltacı, already booked at 16, fouled Vlahovic in a challenge. Atilla Karaoğlan sent him off for a second yellow. Gençlerbirliği were down to ten. The minute is 22 on the beIN log and the club page, and 23 in the beIN introduction.[1][2]

At 45, Murillo played a pass in behind. Trossard entered the area and laid the ball off to Vlahovic, who was beyond the six-yard box. Vlahovic put it into an empty net: 2-0. That was the half-time score.[1][2]

The club log also records a yellow card for Murillo at 33, and a yellow card for Ndidi. Ndidi's minute is not on the numbered list of key moments.[2]

This take: The match looks settled by the interval. The second goal arrived while the visitors were already a man short, and before the half closed. The spine of the 3-0 is there.

Poku at 85, three assists for Trossard

At 85 Ernest Poku made it 3-0. beIN names Leandro Trossard, with three assists, as the other player who stood out. The same report ties the passes for the goals at 20 and 45 to Trossard. It does not describe how the third assist was made.[1][2]

The club timeline lists the changes as follows: 27 Diabate–Thalisson; 70 Kartal–Ndidi, Olaitan–Orkun, Taylan–Amir; 71 Tiago–Luis, Adama–Fıratcan; 77 İlhan–Poku; 86 Semih–Dusan; 87 Salih–Oğulcan, Sekou–Pedro. Diabate was booked at 49. Orkun Kökçü was booked at 55.[2]

This take: 3-0 is not a winning score built against eleven. Poku's 85th closed the scoreboard. Trossard's three assists are a separate line. The 77th-minute entry puts İlhan and Poku on the same line; the club does not say who came on and who went off.

From 19 points to 22

Before the match, Anadolu Ajansı had Beşiktaş on 19 points from 10 matches: 6 wins, 1 draw, 3 defeats. At home the record was five wins from five: Eyüpspor 1-0, Çorum FK 6-2, Erzurumspor FK 3-0, Kocaelispor 2-1, İstanbul Başakşehir 2-0. Fourteen goals scored, three conceded. The same piece summed up the previous five competitive matches as one win, eight scored and nine conceded: Trabzonspor away 0-2, Başakşehir 2-0, Kasımpaşa 2-2, Crystal Palace 2-2, Celtic 3-2. The club page also recalls the previous league match, a 2-2 away draw with Kasımpaşa.[2][3]

beIN, after tonight, has Beşiktaş on 22 points in fourth, and Gençlerbirliği on 11 points in 14th. Anadolu Ajansı had already recorded that Gençlerbirliği reached 11 points by beating Alanyaspor 5-0, failed to score and conceded 11 across the previous three away matches, and that their only away win was 1-0 at Göztepe in matchweek 2. Last season they beat Beşiktaş 2-1 away.[1][3]

Anadolu Ajansı wrote that one yellow card would leave Orkun Kökçü, Emirhan Topçu or Trossard out of the TÜMOSAN Konyaspor match after the international break. On the club log, the name from that trio who was booked is Orkun, at 55. There is no yellow-card line for Emirhan Topçu or Trossard.[2][3]

beIN says that after the international break Beşiktaş visit Tümosan Konyaspor, and Gençlerbirliği host Gaziantep FK.[1]

The table is on the Besiktas standings page, the Genclerbirligi standings page and the Super Lig table.

This take: The pre-match home sentence moves, with this score, from five wins out of five to six out of six. The 8-9 window across the previous five matches looks closed, and it closed against ten men. The disciplinary line Anadolu Ajansı drew now sits on Orkun, via the yellow card at 55. The assist line stays with Trossard.

— futbolradar
