The Derby Starts in Konya: Beşiktaş's Road to the Top

FutbolRadar 09 November 2026

Six points behind the leaders, eleven points dropped away from home.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Two truths at the international break

Beşiktaş went into the international break smiling. They beat Gençlerbirliği 3-0, moved to 22 points and settled into fourth place. Leaders Galatasaray have 28, Trabzonspor 25 and Amed 23. [1]

On paper the picture is clear: we are six points behind the leaders. But behind those six points stand two different Beşiktaş sides. One plays like champions at Dolmabahçe; the other loses itself as soon as it leaves the city.

This piece is about both. Because this season's story will be written by which of these two teams wins out.

Nobody gets past Dolmabahçe

First, the good news. Beşiktaş have won all six of their home league games this season. They scored 17 goals in those games and conceded only 3. Everyone who came to Dolmabahçe left empty-handed. [4]

By winning his first six home league games, Vincenzo Italiano became the first coach in the club's history to do so since Ljubisa Spajic in 1962-63. Dusan Vlahovic has reached nine league goals, and his partnership with Trossard becomes more fluent every week. [2] [1]

And let us not forget: this team went to Kadıköy and won 2-1. In other words, it knew how to win at the hardest away ground of all. There is a title-winning spirit in this side; nobody can deny that any more. [4]

A different team outside the city

Now the worrying part. Beşiktaş have played five away league games and taken only 4 of the 15 points available. They lost at Alanya, at Amed and at Trabzon, and drew 2-2 at Kasımpaşa. They scored 6 goals and conceded 9 in those games. [3] [4]

That is the lowest away points tally at this stage of the season in the last five seasons. 18 from 18 at home, 4 from 15 on the road. Like two different teams wearing the same shirt. [3]

The arithmetic is actually very simple. We are six points behind the leaders, and we have dropped eleven points away from home. In other words, what keeps Beşiktaş from the top is not their rivals but their own journeys. This team's title credentials are tested not at Dolmabahçe but the moment it gets on the bus.

The derby starts in Konya

After the break the calendar is breathtaking. On Sunday 22 November at 16:00 Turkish time, the trip to Konyaspor; on 26 November, Hapoel Beer-Sheva in the Europa League; and on Monday 30 November at 21:00, the Galatasaray derby at Dolmabahçe. Then on 6 December Samsunspor come to our ground. [4]

Everyone's eyes will be on 30 November. But the real test comes eight days earlier, in Konya. An opponent waiting near the bottom of the table on eight points, and captain Orkun suspended on top of that. Exactly the kind of game in which Beşiktaş have dropped points this season. [1]

So here is the question worth thinking about: would the derby still be a title derby for a Beşiktaş that comes back from Konya empty-handed? A Beşiktaş that wins in Konya and then wins the derby, on the other hand, would cut the gap to the leaders to three points at most. It is not Dolmabahçe but Konya that will decide what the derby means.

Eagle, make the road your nest too

We have seen what this team can do. It turned Dolmabahçe into a fortress, knew how to win at Kadıköy, found goals with Vlahovic and ideas with Trossard. The only thing missing is packing that courage in a suitcase and taking it on the road.

As supporters, our job is clear. On the night of 30 November we will bring Dolmabahçe down; nobody doubts that. But what turns that night into a title night will be the step taken in Konya on the afternoon of 22 November.

The gap is six points, and there are 23 weeks ahead of us. This race is not over; it has only just begun. If the Eagle that learned to win at home now learns to win on the road too, nobody can write the end of this season in advance. Wings spread, course set for the top. Come on, Beşiktaş!
