The Nets Won't Stay Quiet at Papara Park: A Goal Rush Awaits in the Trabzonspor–Beşiktaş Derby

FutbolRadar 19 October 2026

Trabzonspor–Beşiktaş is tonight at 20:00 at Papara Park. There were six goals here last season: we think the nets won't stay quiet tonight.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

Some games are written in the calendar, others in the heart. The Trabzonspor–Beşiktaş game at Papara Park tonight at 20:00 is the second kind. It is the closing game of Trendyol Süper Lig matchweek 8, and Cihan Aydın will referee. [1][2] The wild waves of the Black Sea and Istanbul's black-and-white storm meet for the 144th time. [7]

I don't want to open this column with a question; I'm opening it with a claim: the nets won't stay quiet at Papara Park tonight. This is not a hunch, it is a fact the numbers are shouting. Let's look at those numbers together.

Two hungry wolves at the table

Beşiktaş go into the derby 4th in the league on 15 points, with 5 wins and 2 defeats from 7 games. Vincenzo Italiano's team have scored 16 and conceded 8. Trabzonspor have collected 13 points from 7 games with 4 wins, 1 draw and 2 defeats, and are 5th. The claret-and-blues have also scored 16 but conceded only 5; the meanest defence in the league. [2][6]

Now look at this: both teams score 2.29 goals per game. Beşiktaş's games have averaged 3.43 goals, Trabzonspor's 3.00. The 63 games played in the first 7 matchweeks produced 185 goals in total, a league average of 2.94. Tonight two teams who play higher-scoring games than the league average take the field. [2][3]

Let's also look at the threshold known as "over 2.5", meaning games with 3 or more goals. At least 3 goals were scored in 5 of Beşiktaş's 7 league games and in 5 of Trabzonspor's 7. In 10 of the two teams' combined 14 league games, the net bulged at least 3 times. [2]

Form: one has taken flight, the other has been reborn

Beşiktaş have 4 wins and 1 defeat from their last 5 league games (W-W-W-L-W): they beat Çorum 6-2, Fenerbahçe 2-1 away, Erzurumspor 3-0 and Kocaelispor 2-1, and lost 3-2 away to Amedspor. They scored 15 goals in those five games. [2] After the international break, they followed Kocaelispor by beating Hoffenheim in the Europa League, putting together a two-game winning run. [10][16]

Trabzonspor's last 5 games produced 3 wins and 2 defeats (L-W-L-W-W). [2] But the real story is the last two weeks: with Thomas Reis's arrival, the claret-and-blues first crushed Galatasaray 4-0 at Papara Park, then won 3-0 away to Samsunspor. [2][13] In Europe too, Trabzonspor beat KuPS 4-2 in Finland, winning all three competitive games under Reis. [10][11][14][15] The hosts have won all 3 league games they have played at Papara Park this season, scoring 11 goals in them. [2]

To be honest, the numbers have another side too: Trabzonspor have conceded only 1 goal in 3 games at home. But in two of those 3 games they scored a total of 9 goals against Gençlerbirliği and Galatasaray. This team does not just shut the door at home; it goes at its opponents. Beşiktaş, meanwhile, have failed to keep a clean sheet in any of their 3 away league games. [2] A visiting side that cannot keep clean sheets and a home side that cannot stop scoring... The formula is familiar.

The strikers: a Salah v Vlahović duel

The two brightest stars of this derby are in the forward lines. According to the TFF scoring chart, Trabzonspor's Mohamed Salah is among the leaders with 7 goals. Beşiktaş's Dušan Vlahović is right behind on 6. For Trabzonspor, Ernest Muçi, Franculino and Noah Saviolo have 2 each; for Beşiktaş, İlhan Fakılı, Václav Černý and Orkun Kökçü have also contributed 2 goals each. [4]

Absentees and probable XIs

For Beşiktaş, captain Orkun Kökçü, injured against Hoffenheim, and Milot Rashica, who is still in treatment, will miss out. For Trabzonspor, Malinovskyi, Batagov, Okay Yokuşlu, Onuachu and young goalkeeper Onuralp Çevikkan are injured; Melih Kabasakal, sent off against Samsunspor, is suspended. [10][11]

Different versions of the probable XIs are circulating in the press; the final line-ups will become clear closer to kick-off. [9][11]

The absence of the two creative brains in midfield, Kökçü and Malinovskyi, could make the game less controlled, more disjointed and more open. In derbies, the less control there is, the more likely goals become.

The rivalry: Papara Park's memory for goals

The rivalry's recent memory feeds this claim too. Last season's game at Papara Park finished 3-3. [7][12] That night Beşiktaş were down to 10 men from the 38th minute, and Trabzonspor took 34 shots in a single game; six goals and a breathtaking 90 minutes. [12]

History in Trabzon says the same thing: the two teams have met 52 times in Trabzon in the Süper Lig. Trabzonspor have won 24, Beşiktaş 15, and 13 games ended in draws. In those games Trabzonspor scored 75 and Beşiktaş 65; a total of 140 goals, roughly 2.7 per game. [7][8]

The most striking data lies in the rivalry's recent past. The last 10 competitive meetings were as follows: [17]

9 May 2026 · Süper Lig · Beşiktaş 1-2 Trabzonspor (3 goals)

14 December 2025 · Süper Lig · Trabzonspor 3-3 Beşiktaş (6 goals)

15 February 2025 · Süper Lig · Beşiktaş 2-1 Trabzonspor (3 goals)

15 September 2024 · Süper Lig · Trabzonspor 1-1 Beşiktaş (2 goals)

23 May 2024 · Turkish Cup final · Beşiktaş 3-2 Trabzonspor (5 goals)

4 February 2024 · Süper Lig · Beşiktaş 2-0 Trabzonspor (2 goals)

17 September 2023 · Süper Lig · Trabzonspor 3-0 Beşiktaş (3 goals)

16 April 2023 · Süper Lig · Trabzonspor 0-0 Beşiktaş (0 goals)

16 October 2022 · Süper Lig · Beşiktaş 2-2 Trabzonspor (4 goals)

3 April 2022 · Süper Lig · Trabzonspor 1-1 Beşiktaş (2 goals)

These 10 games produced 30 goals, exactly 3.0 per game. 6 of the 10 had 3 or more goals, and in 7 both teams scored. Narrow it down to the last 5 games and the picture heats up further: 19 goals, 3.8 per game. Both teams scored in all five of those games, and four of them had at least 3 goals.

The picture doesn't change across the whole history either: in 143 games Beşiktaş have won 57 and Trabzonspor 49, with 37 draws; Beşiktaş have scored 199 and Trabzonspor 165. [7][8] So tonight Beşiktaş are looking for their 200th goal in this rivalry. A team standing on the edge of such a milestone does not want to leave the pitch without scoring it.

Closing: my prediction

Let's sum up. On one side, a Trabzonspor who shut their door, crushed Galatasaray 4-0 and have been reborn under Reis, with Salah at the top of the scoring race. On the other, a Beşiktaş whose games average 3.43 goals, who have yet to keep a clean sheet away from home, and who are rising on Vlahović's back. The players considered the brains of both teams are on the sidelines; in the middle there are two great strikers and a rivalry that produced six goals at Papara Park last season.

My prediction is clear and I stand by it: tonight at Papara Park we will watch a high-scoring derby. I expect at least 3 goals, both teams to find the net and a breathtaking 90 minutes right up to the final whistle. Who will win I don't know, it's a derby; but I'm sure the stands will be on their feet again and again tonight.

This is one writer's opinion, not a certainty. But when the numbers speak this loudly, staying quiet is not for me.
