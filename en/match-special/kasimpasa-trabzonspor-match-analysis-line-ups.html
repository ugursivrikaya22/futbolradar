Salah travels, but is not expected to start

Trabzonspor's new signing Mohamed Salah was included in the squad that travelled to Istanbul for the Kasımpaşa match. Pre-match coverage expects him to start on the bench rather than in the XI, with minutes later in the game. This is a reported expectation rather than a club statement. It will be settled when the teamsheet lands — and at that moment this panel gives way to the official line-up.

A midfield three shaped by absentees

Both Okay Yokuşlu and Tim Jabol-Folcarelli are out. That means the midfield three is shaped by necessity rather than preference: Ozan Tufan, Bouchouari and Malinovskyi. The balance is notable. Malinovskyi pushes on, Bouchouari links, and Ozan Tufan can both win the ball and arrive late in the box. But without a natural holding midfielder, who takes charge of transition defence is an open question.

Service to Onuachu

Paul Onuachu's height and presence in the box give Trabzonspor a natural reference point in attack. Crosses from wide and second balls can be expected to gather around him. That is also a risk: an attack built around a single reference becomes predictable for a defence. Saviolo and Aral Şimşir looking for depth from wide areas is the way to break that predictability.

Muçi and Batagov are also missing

Ernest Muçi and Arseniy Batagov are unavailable alongside Okay Yokuşlu and Jabol-Folcarelli. Four absentees narrow the options in attack and defence alike. Savić and Nwaiwu are expected at centre-back; with Batagov out, the understanding of that pairing is tested straight away on the opening weekend.
