Key Matchups

Kasımpaşa – Trabzonspor · The individual duels most likely to decide the match.

Onuachu vs Kasımpaşa's centre-backs

The most intense duel of the match will be inside the box. Onuachu's height and use of his body place a constant burden on defenders in the air.

The centre-backs' job is not only to win headers but to stop a striker turning with his back to goal, and to occupy the area where second balls drop. In contests like this the second ball is often more decisive than the first.

Which pairing Kasımpaşa field has not been announced; Kamil Ahmet Çörekçi's injury narrows the options at the back.

Kasımpaşa's attack vs the space in front of Trabzonspor's defence

This duel is about a zone rather than a player — and it may be the most decisive of the match. Trabzonspor's midfield, built without a natural holder, must share responsibility in transition.

If Kasımpaşa can get the ball into that zone regularly, Trabzonspor's centre-backs are pulled into one-against-one duels outside the box. Savić and Nwaiwu facing that test on the opening weekend will answer quickly how settled the pairing is.

Whether the hosts have the player profiles to attack that zone is unknown, since their squad has not been announced.

Malinovskyi's set pieces vs Kasımpaşa's box defending

In a tight match, set pieces can be the only thing that decides it. Malinovskyi's delivery combined with Onuachu's presence in the box is a direct route to goal for Trabzonspor.

The hosts' answer will be organising the near post correctly and setting their zonal assignments according to who can win the ball in the air. That is a question of organisation — the area where individual quality matters least.

No set-piece numbers are quoted on this page: on the opening weekend of a season, no such sample exists.

Salah's possible introduction vs a tiring Kasımpaşa defence

Salah being in the squad opens a separate heading for the final half hour. An attacker introduced from the bench meets a defence that has already run for ninety minutes, and the threshold is different.

When and how that duel happens depends on the scoreline. With Trabzonspor ahead, his introduction manages the game; level or behind, it tries to open it.

This entire section rests on reported expectation: his inclusion in the squad is confirmed, whether he plays is not.
