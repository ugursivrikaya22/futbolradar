Strengths and Weaknesses

Gençlerbirliği – Fenerbahçe · Four strengths and four weaknesses for each side, all grounded in the verified squad situation.

Fenerbahçe — strengths

• Variety in attack: İrfan Can Kahveci, Asensio, Oğuz Aydın and Talisca each threaten from different areas, so a defence never gets a fixed reference point. • Aerial command at centre-back: Škriniar and Aké can protect the box in open play and at set pieces alike. • Midfield balance: the Guendouzi–İsmail Yüksek pairing links ball-winning to forward play. • Match rhythm: a side that has already played four competitive fixtures in Champions League qualifying does not enter the league cold.

Fenerbahçe — weaknesses

• An unfamiliar goalkeeping situation: Ederson suspended, Günok injured. On the opening league weekend, the communication in front of the defence is not routine. • Uncertainty at left-back: Oosterwolde's absence means rebuilding both the defensive balance and the left-sided relationship. • The price of high full-backs: with both pushed on, the rest defence drops to three. • Fixture pressure: the Lyon play-off is three days away, and squad management can override the logic of the game itself.

Gençlerbirliği — strengths

• Home advantage: the atmosphere at Eryaman is a real factor, especially early on. • Managerial continuity: the stability found under Metin Diyadin at the end of last season is a valuable starting point for a club that changed manager five times. • No weight of expectation: not being favourites against Fenerbahçe on the opening weekend simplifies the game plan. • A squad refreshed in the summer: four new names plus the agreement with Salih Uçan are direct additions to last season's group.

Gençlerbirliği — weaknesses

• Last season's table: finishing 14th, just above the drop, points to fragile defensive stability. • The record in this fixture: no home win over Fenerbahçe in eleven years, with the last meeting lost 3-1. • Squad uncertainty: no verified match-specific squad information is available, and the injuries reported by some outlets could not be confirmed. • Attacking departures: losing Onyekuru and Niang removes two players from last season's forward line.
