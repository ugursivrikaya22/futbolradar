Strengths and Weaknesses

Kasımpaşa – Trabzonspor · Four strengths and four weaknesses for each side, grounded in the verified squad situation.

Trabzonspor — strengths

• Physical superiority in the box: Onuachu is a constant threat in open play and at set pieces. • Experience in goal: André Onana has played at the highest level. • Creativity in midfield: Malinovskyi's final ball and set-piece delivery can be the difference in a tight match. • A new option in reserve: Salah on the bench offers a distinct plan for the second half.

Trabzonspor — weaknesses

• Two of four absentees are central midfielders: without Okay Yokuşlu and Jabol-Folcarelli, they must play without a natural holder. • A partnership under examination: with Batagov out, Savić and Nwaiwu are tested immediately on the opening weekend. • Pre-season return: one win in four friendlies suggests they do not enter the season at their most fluent. • Predictability in attack: built around a single reference point, the attack becomes easier for a defence to read.

Kasımpaşa — strengths

• Home advantage: opening the season at their own ground makes it easier to impose a structure. • The opponent's midfield absences: Trabzonspor's two missing central midfielders leave the hosts a concrete target zone. • A refreshed squad: nine new signings are a direct attempt to move beyond last season's limits. • No weight of expectation: not being favourites simplifies the game plan.

Kasımpaşa — weaknesses

• Time to settle: a squad that added nine players may not yet be a unit on the opening weekend. • One defensive absentee: Kamil Ahmet Çörekçi's injury narrows the options at the back. • A potential physical mismatch in the box: aerial duels against Onuachu may be where the defence is stretched most. • The historical record: 240 wins in 786 league matches reflects a club that has spent most of its top-flight seasons in the lower half.
