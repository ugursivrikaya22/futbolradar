Tactical Analysis

Kasımpaşa – Trabzonspor · Expected shapes, midfield balance, transition moments and set pieces.

Expected shapes

Trabzonspor are expected in a 4-3-3: Onana in goal; Pina, Nwaiwu, Savić and Lopes Cabral across the back; Ozan Tufan, Bouchouari and Malinovskyi in midfield; Saviolo, Onuachu and Aral Şimşir ahead of them.

There is no verified information on Kasımpaşa's shape — no probable XI was published. A compact setup would be a reasonable expectation from a home side under Emre Belözoğlu, but that is an inference; nothing can be stated firmly until the teamsheet lands.

The decisive point will be how Trabzonspor's midfield three, built without a natural holder, behaves in transition.

The balance of the midfield

Okay Yokuşlu's absence is the most concrete tactical consequence here. A holding midfielder's job is not only to win the ball but to delay the first pass of a counter, collect second balls and screen the space in front of the centre-backs.

That job is likely to be shared between Ozan Tufan and Bouchouari. Shared responsibility gives flexibility when it works; when it does not, the responsibility falls between them and the very zone in front of the centre-backs opens up.

Targeting that zone may be Kasımpaşa's most realistic route to creating at home.

Transitions

Trabzonspor looking for depth through their wide players requires the full-backs to push on too. When the ball is lost, the rest defence comes down to Savić, Nwaiwu and one midfielder.

There is an opportunity for Kasımpaşa there: win the ball, release it wide immediately and lengthen the distance Trabzonspor have to recover. That requires pace and runners in the final third — and the home side's forward line for this match has not been announced.

In the other direction, how quickly Trabzonspor can reach Onuachu on the counter may be their most productive weapon.

Set pieces

Onuachu's presence in the box makes Trabzonspor's set pieces a natural threat. How the hosts organise their near-post defending at corners and wide free kicks may be decisive.

Malinovskyi's set-piece delivery is a separate heading in that picture; but we have no verified set-piece statistics for this fixture, and on the opening weekend of a season no such sample exists.

For Kasımpaşa, set plays may be the way to find a balance they cannot build in open play — the most concrete tool for testing the visitors' defensive organisation.
