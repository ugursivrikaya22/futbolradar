Kasımpaşa's Game Plan

Kasımpaşa – Trabzonspor · Belözoğlu's rebuilt squad, home advantage and a 23rd Süper Lig season.

A squad substantially rebuilt

Kasımpaşa added nine players in the summer: Güven Yalçın, Thiemoko Diarra, Ayberk Karapo, Marcus Rafferty, Elson Mendes, Matei Ilie, Jakob Jessen, Kenan Kurtovic and Stann Elysee Dalo. They also made Adrian Benedyczak's loan from last season permanent.

A rebuild of that scale is both an opportunity and a risk on the opening weekend. New squads usually settle over the first months rather than the first weeks.

The XI Emre Belözoğlu will field has not been announced, so no projected shape is offered here.

A 23rd season at home

This match begins Kasımpaşa's 23rd Süper Lig season. Across those campaigns the club's league record stands at 240 wins, 214 draws and 332 defeats in 786 matches.

Those figures describe a club's standing in the division, not the outcome of this match. The value of three home points on the opening weekend lies in banking them before the table settles.

The relatively tight dimensions of the Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Stadium can help a home side that sets up compactly.

Targeting Trabzonspor's midfield

If Trabzonspor start without a natural holding midfielder, the most concrete target zone for the hosts is the space in front of the centre-backs.

The way to attack it is to play forward immediately after winning possession and lengthen the recovery distance for the midfield. That requires a forward line that runs rather than waits.

Whether Kasımpaşa make that choice is unknown; neither their squad nor their shape has been announced.
