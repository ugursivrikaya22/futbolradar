Besiktas beat Genclerbirligi 3-0 and move to 22 points

Anadolu Ajansı's 8 November 2026 report said Besiktas beat Genclerbirligi 3-0 in week 11 of the Super Lig. Vlahovic scored on 20 and 45 minutes and Poku on 85.

BesiktasGenclerbirligiSuper Lig

Besiktas beat Genclerbirligi 3-0 at home in week 11. Anadolu Ajansı said Dusan Vlahovic scored in the 20th minute from Leandro Trossard's pass and again in the 45th minute after another Trossard ball.

Genclerbirligi went down to 10 men in the 22nd minute. The agency wrote that Metehan Baltaci, already booked, fouled Vlahovic and referee Atilla Karaoglan showed a second yellow card. Ernest Poku touched home Trossard's chip in the 85th minute for 3-0.

Anadolu Ajansı's weekend roundup said Besiktas moved to 22 points and entered the international break in fourth. Genclerbirligi coach Bernd Storck said he congratulated Besiktas and thought they deserved the win. The TFF table lists Besiktas on 22 points and Genclerbirligi 14th on 11.
