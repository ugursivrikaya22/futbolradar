Cevad Prekazi dies aged 69

Anadolu Ajansı and Galatasaray reported on 8-9 November 2026 that Cevad Prekazi died in Belgrade aged 69. The club published a condolence message for the player born on 18 August 1957.

GalatasarayCevad PrekaziObituary

Former Galatasaray forward Cevad Prekazi has died. Anadolu Ajansı reported on 8 November 2026 that Partizan's message read that they thanked him for everything and wished him to rest in peace, and that the time and place of the funeral would be announced later.

On 9 November Anadolu Ajansı carried Galatasaray's condolence message and wrote that Prekazi was born in Mitrovica, Kosovo, on 18 August 1957, died in Belgrade on Sunday 8 November aged 69, won two league titles with Galatasaray and scored 40 goals.

On its own website Galatasaray said Prekazi played for the club from 1985 to 1991, scored 40 goals in 185 matches, and scored from about 40 metres against Monaco in the 51st minute in 1989. The club also said he later played for Altay and Bakırköyspor.

FutbolRadar note: the age used here is 69, as stated by the club and by Anadolu Ajansı on 9 November, with a birth date of 18 August 1957.
