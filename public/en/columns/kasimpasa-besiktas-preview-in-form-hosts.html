Kasımpaşa – Beşiktaş: In-Form Hosts, Visitors Looking to Solve Their Away Problem

FutbolRadar 31 October 2026

There are 2 points between them: Kasımpaşa, with 4 wins, 4 draws and 1 defeat, host a Beşiktaş side struggling away from home on 1 November at 19:00.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

In Süper Lig matchweek 10, Kasımpaşa host Beşiktaş at the Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Stadium on Sunday 1 November at 19:00. The game will be shown on beIN SPORTS 1, and the referee is Reşat Onur Coşkunses [1]. On paper there are only 2 points between the two teams: Beşiktaş are 4th on 18 points, Kasımpaşa 7th on 16 [1][2]. But the two teams have got here in very different ways.

Form: consistency or fluctuation?

Kasımpaşa have 4 wins, 4 draws and 1 defeat from 9 league games [1]. Their only defeat came away to Galatasaray in matchweek 7 (3-1). They then beat Samsunspor 4-1 at home, and last week came back from Eyüpspor with a 1-0 win [1].

Their last three league games:

Galatasaray 3-1 Kasımpaşa

Kasımpaşa 4-1 Samsunspor

Eyüpspor 0-1 Kasımpaşa

Beşiktaş have 6 wins and 3 defeats from 9 games; they have yet to draw [2]. The black-and-whites look strong at home but struggle on the road. Of their four away games this season they have won only the Fenerbahçe derby, 2-1; they lost 1-0 to Alanyaspor, 3-2 to Amed Sportif Faaliyetler and 2-0 to Trabzonspor. They have taken only 3 of the 12 points available away from home [2]. Last week they beat Başakşehir 2-0 at home [1].

This is my reading: Beşiktaş's place in the table is good, but their results differ markedly between home and away. This game will show whether that picture changes.

Recent meetings: a history in Kasımpaşa's favour

In the recent past this fixture has not been comfortable for Beşiktaş. According to AA's round-up, the black-and-whites have won only 1 of their last 5 league games against Kasımpaşa [2]:

2023-24: Beşiktaş lost 3-1 and 2-1 to Kasımpaşa [3]

2024-25: Beşiktaş lost 3-1 at home and drew 1-1 away [2]

2025-26: 1-1 away at Kasımpaşa, Beşiktaş won 2-1 at the Tüpraş Stadium [2][3]

In last season's 2-1 game, Beşiktaş's goals came from Oh and Orkun Kökçü, and Kasımpaşa's from a Benedyczak penalty [3]. In the same game goalkeeper Gianniotis saved two clear chances from Cengiz Ünder [3].

Key players and absentees

Beşiktaş's biggest loss is captain Orkun Kökçü. According to an AA-sourced report, Orkun, whose injury continues, and Fabio Miretti are not expected to be in the squad [2]. Ajansspor also lists Rashica, Cerny and Ümit among the injured alongside Orkun and Miretti; Can Keleş is also among the absentees [1]. If Emirhan Topçu and Leandro Trossard are booked, they will be suspended for next week's game against Gençlerbirliği [2].

For Kasımpaşa, according to Ajansspor's list, Kamil Ahmet, Frimpong, Ben Ouanes and Diabate are injured; there are no suspended players [1].

Ajansspor's probable XIs (not official line-ups, the outlet's prediction):

Kasımpaşa: Gianniotis, Winck, Arous, Ilie, Ayberk, Kerem, Baldursson, Mendes, Rak-Sakyi, Ali Yavuz, Benedyczak [1]

Beşiktaş: Nübel, Murillo, Agbadou, Emirhan, Rıdvan, Salih, Olaitan, Ndidi, Poku, Trossard, Vlahovic [1]

Tactical summary

This is my reading: without Orkun and Miretti, Beşiktaş are likely to put more responsibility on Ndidi and Salih in midfield; part of the build-up can be expected to shift to the wings, especially to Trossard and Olaitan.

This is my reading: Kasımpaşa, as they have shown in recent weeks, may keep the score level and rely on Benedyczak's finishing and transitions down the flanks in attack. For the hosts, Winck, Mendes, Kerem and Ali Yavuz have each been booked 3 times this season [1]; that calls for care in physical duels.

Referee Coşkunses has shown 9 yellow cards and 1 straight red in 4 games this season, and has not awarded a penalty [1].

Conclusion: what is at stake in the table?

According to AA's report, Beşiktaş want to win this game to keep up their pursuit of leaders Galatasaray, who came through the matchweek without dropping points [2]. For Kasımpaşa, a win would mean overtaking Beşiktaş on points; the hosts, on 16 points, would move to 19, above Beşiktaş's 18 [1].

This is my reading: although the betting market makes Beşiktaş clear favourites [1], their weak away results and their recent history against Kasımpaşa call that favouritism into question. With their depleted midfield, an early goal for Beşiktaş could decide the game; Kasımpaşa's chances grow the longer they keep it level.
