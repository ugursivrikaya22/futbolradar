The January Window Is Coming: The Transfers Being Talked About, From the Süper Lig to Europe

FutbolRadar 10 November 2026

None of it is official: we sorted the transfer talk before the January window, from the Süper Lig to Europe, onto three shelves.

Until an official announcement arrives, every transfer story is only a possibility.

FutbolRadar

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Update · 11 November 2026 at 20:45

A sourced quote from Sergen Yalçın's 11 November press conference, about Enes Ünal starting as centre-forward against Belgium, has been added to the opening of this piece.

The table is set before the window opens

The January transfer window is still weeks away. But with the pitches quiet during the international break, the negotiating tables have started talking. From İstanbul to Trabzon, from Madrid to London, a new name and a new figure do the rounds every day.

During the same international break, at the 11 November press conference before the Belgium match, Türkiye head coach Sergen Yalçın said: "You were asking whether there was no centre-forward — I brought one with me; there is. Enes will start, the centre-forward is with us" (translated from Turkish) [16]. The remark was that Enes Ünal would start at centre-forward against Belgium; it was not a club-transfer claim.

In this piece we have gathered the transfer talk of recent weeks in one place. First, a warning: none of the stories below is an official announcement. They are all claims, contacts and shortlists reported by the press. That is how we read them too: which ones grow out of a real need, which are genuinely being discussed, and which are just headlines?

Süper Lig: the big four's shortlists

Galatasaray: a striker first. Osimhen is out for about six weeks after surgery, and Rafael Leão will be sidelined for four to six weeks [1]. For Galatasaray, finding a striker is therefore not a luxury but a necessity. According to Fanatik, Swiss forward Winsley Boteli, who could not be signed in the summer, is back on the agenda. The 20-year-old Sion player has 13 goals and 5 assists in 16 games in all competitions, and the fact that he fits the under-23 quota is a bonus [2]. Stuttgart's Bosnian striker Ermedin Demirovic is also on the list; Leeds United are said to be interested too [3].

In midfield there is a familiar name: Christ Inao Oulai, the 20-year-old who left Trabzonspor for Fiorentina for €26 million. According to Milliyet, Galatasaray have made first contact with the player [4]. There could be traffic in the other direction too: the Italian press says Fiorentina will knock on Galatasaray's door for Lucas Torreira in January [5]. The departures of Bitshiabu and Ugochukwu are also being discussed to free up quota places [2].

Fenerbahçe: Emre's first list. New Fenerbahçe coach Emre Belözoğlu has asked for two names. One is Massire Sylla, Union Saint-Gilloise's 21-year-old centre-back; the other is Danilo, Botafogo's 25-year-old Brazilian midfielder [6]. Danilo has scored 13 goals in 39 games this season. But the price is steep: against Atalanta's offer of €22 million plus €8 million, Botafogo are reported to want €40 million net [6].

Beşiktaş: a captain for the defence. Italiano's Beşiktaş conceded twice against Hoffenheim, Trabzonspor, Crystal Palace and Kasımpaşa. The target follows from that: Genoa's Mexican captain Johan Vásquez. The 28-year-old can play at centre-back or left-back and is valued at around €14 million [7].

Trabzonspor: four positions. Trabzonspor are looking to strengthen in midfield, up front, at centre-back and at right-back. Häcken are reported to want €15 million for their 19-year-old holding midfielder Abdoulaye Doumbia, and Estrela's Eddy Doué is also being tracked. Up front there is a surprise name: Tottenham's Richarlison. Tottenham are said to have halved their asking terms for a transfer or a loan [8].

Europe: a teenager, a centre-back hunt and a search for goals

Real Madrid's 16-year-old coup. Europe's most talked-about file concerns a teenager: JJ Gabriel, 16, from Manchester United's academy. The attacking midfielder, who asked to leave in September, has chosen Real Madrid over Barcelona [9]. The most striking part is the money: United value him at up to £100 million, but because it is a move abroad they will receive only FIFA training compensation, which is not expected to exceed $400,000 [9]. The Spanish press expects the deal to be completed within two weeks [10].

Barcelona want a centre-back. The Catalan club's name is linked with a centre-back almost every day: Bayern's Jonathan Tah, Inter's Alessandro Bastoni, Chelsea's Levi Colwill. The Spanish press even reported that Virgil van Dijk wants a move to Barcelona [11][12]. The same reports, however, say Bayern have no intention of selling Tah in January [12]. In the other direction, Chelsea's €50 million interest in Jules Koundé is being talked about [11].

Arsenal want a goalscorer. Arteta's side have won six of their nine league wins by a single goal, and Havertz, Ødegaard and Saka are on three league goals each [13]. According to reports citing the BBC, the first target is Bayer Leverkusen's 20-year-old Cameroonian forward Christian Kofane [13]. Club Brugge's Carlos Forbs is also on the list [10]. And that list includes the most important name for us: Kenan Yıldız.

Turkish stars in the shop window

According to Milliyet and Sözcü, relaying a Daily Mirror report, Arsenal plan to open formal talks with Juventus over Kenan Yıldız in January [14][15]. Arteta is looking for a versatile forward who can also play on the left. The 21-year-old has 27 goals and 21 assists in 132 competitive games for Juventus [14][15].

But there are two question marks. The first is fitness: because of injury, Kenan has played only once this season [14]. The second is Juventus: the reports mention neither a fee nor the Italian club's stance. So what is on the table today is an intention, not an offer. Among Turkish players, Kenan ranks second only to Arda Güler in market value.

The Oulai file runs the other way: a young player who left the Süper Lig for Serie A is being talked about as returning to Türkiye only months later. Oulai, who has yet to score or assist in nine league games for Fiorentina, is said to be open to coming back [4].

Verdict: which is real, which is a headline?

I think this list belongs on three shelves.

The concrete ones. JJ Gabriel's move to Real Madrid looks all but settled given the player's own stance [9][10]. In Trabzonspor's Doumbia file, the figure is known and the meeting schedule is known [8]. These are transfers genuinely being discussed.

The ones born of need. Galatasaray's search for a striker and Beşiktaş's search for a centre-back follow naturally from what is happening on the pitch. Osimhen's absence and the goals Beşiktaş have conceded make these stories logical [1][7]. The names may change, but it would be no surprise if these two teams signed players in those positions in January.

The expensive dreams. A €40 million Danilo, or sitting down with Juventus over an injured Kenan Yıldız: exciting headlines, but for now they are intentions [6][14]. And then there is the Grealish example: in the same week one report described Atlético's interest, and another said the loan would not be cut short [11][12]. On the road to January we will see many more contradictions like that.

Transfer windows are a season of hope for supporters. But one rule helps when reading: until an official announcement arrives, every story is only a possibility. In January we will see which names actually sign; on that day we can open this piece again and see together which shelf proved right.
