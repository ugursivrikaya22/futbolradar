The Same 16 Points, Nine Goals Apart on Goal Difference

FutbolRadar 08 November 2026

A draw would leave the order on 16 points exactly as goal difference has it.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

Çorum FK face Fenerbahçe in matchweek 11 of the Süper Lig at the Çorum City Stadium at 16:00 on Sunday 8 November. The game has not yet been played. The referee is Abdullah Buğra Taşkınsoy. Full details are on the match page.[1][8][9]

FotMob lists the venue as the Çorum City Stadium: capacity 15,000, grass pitch, forecast temperature 5°C. The same page gives Abdullah Taşkınsoy averages of 3.3 yellow cards and 23.9 fouls per game. The two teams are meeting for the first time in a competitive match.[1][6]

16 points, three places, nine goals

Matchweek 10 ended on 2 November. Fenerbahçe 6th, Çorum FK 7th, Kocaelispor 8th. All three have 5 wins, 1 draw, 4 defeats and 16 points from 10 games. Fenerbahçe have scored 24 and conceded 10, goal difference +14. Çorum FK have scored 21 and conceded 16, goal difference +5. Kocaelispor have scored 11 and conceded 9, goal difference +2. The gap in goal difference between Fenerbahçe and Çorum is 9 goals.[2]

AA reports that Çorum aim to win at home and move ahead of their opponents. A draw would not change either side's goal difference; Çorum overtaking Fenerbahçe depends on a win.[1][2]

This table is the close of matchweek 10. Both teams play their matchweek 11 game at 16:00 on 8 November; 16 points is the figure after 10 games. For the table, see Fenerbahçe standings, Çorum FK standings and Süper Lig standings.[2]

Four games under Burak, only one at home

Çorum's 10 league games: 2-2 away at Galatasaray, 0-1 at home to Kasımpaşa, 2-6 away at Beşiktaş, 3-0 at home to Eyüpspor, 5-1 away at Samsunspor, 1-2 at home to Alanyaspor. They parted ways with Uğur Uçar on 24 September. Burak Yılmaz started work on 25 September. His first test was a 3-0 win away at Gaziantep. Then a 2-1 home win over Rizespor, a 0-2 defeat away at Göztepe, and a 3-1 win away at Erzurumspor. Four games: 3 wins, 1 defeat, 9 points. The only home game among those four was the 2-1 against Rizespor.[3]

They have played four home league games this season: the Kasımpaşa defeat, Eyüpspor 3-0, the Alanyaspor defeat, Rizespor 2-1. The first three came before Burak. The season's full record is on our Çorum FK matches page.[3]

In FotMob's last five Çorum results they scored 9 goals. The scores as listed on the page: Erzurumspor FK 1-3 Çorum FK, Göztepe 2-0 Çorum FK, Çorum FK 2-1 Rizespor, Gaziantep FK 0-3 Çorum FK, Çorum FK 1-2 Alanyaspor. Fenerbahçe's last five produced 10 goals: Fenerbahçe 3-2 Liverpool, Fenerbahçe 1-2 Göztepe, Galatasaray 2-1 Fenerbahçe, Fenerbahçe 2-2 Slavia Prague, Fenerbahçe 3-0 Alanyaspor.[6]

AA reports that Fenerbahçe have lost their last two league games.[1] Göztepe won 2-1 away at Fenerbahçe; they were 1-0 down at half-time, and the goals came from substitutes Armstrong and Henrique.[5] The 3-2 win over Liverpool came in the Champions League, during Dirk Kuyt's interim spell.[1][4] Fenerbahçe's list of matches is here.

Hrvoje Smolcic points out that they have taken 9 points from the last 4 games under Burak. Ylber Ramadani says Fenerbahçe are a quality side whatever their league position.[4]

The last XI is not Sunday's XI

The starting line-ups have not been announced. FotMob's last XI has Çorum in a 4-4-2 and Fenerbahçe in a 4-2-3-1. That line-up includes Gökhan Sazdağı for Çorum and Archie Brown for Fenerbahçe; both are suspended for this game. The Fenerbahçe coach line still reads Dirk Kuyt. Vedat Muriqi is a substitute on that list, and Romelu Lukaku is in the starting XI.[6]

AA's 7 November note lists three Fenerbahçe absentees: the suspended Archie Brown, Marco Asensio, whose injury continues, and Nathan Ake, who has recovered from injury but is not expected in the squad because he has not started training with the team. For Çorum, Fredy's injury continues and Gökhan Sazdağı is suspended for bookings.[1]

FotMob breaks it down like this: Sazdağı suspended for the 8 November game, Fredy doubtful with a muscle injury, Feyttullah Gürlük out with a muscle injury and due back in mid-November. Brown's ban runs until 22 November. Asensio knee, due back in early January 2027. Nathan Aké hamstring, due back in mid-November. The page also lists Amara Diouf as doubtful with a muscle injury and Jayden Oosterwolde with an Achilles injury.[6]

At Çorum's 5 November training session, Fredy was the only absentee. That sentence refers to training; the suspension is a separate line.[4]

T24 reports that Muriqi has lost his starting place to Lukaku in recent weeks, that he scored the winning goal against Liverpool, and that he is expected to return to the starting XI in Belözoğlu's first game. In tactical work the 32-year-old was with the first team. 17 competitive games, 9 goals, 2 assists.[7] FotMob's Süper Lig line draws on a different pool: Muriqi 7 goals in 10 games, 4.28 expected goals, 1 assist, rating 7.35. Ramírez 6 goals in 10 games, 4.15 expected goals, 1 assist, rating 7.10. Alexandros Kyziridis is the player creating the most big chances at Çorum: 4. Ermin Mahmić averages 3.3 shots on target per game.[6]

The squad was in Çorum at 17:48 on 7 November. The game is at 16:00 the next day.[8]

The first competitive date

Emre Belözoğlu is 46 and takes charge of his first game, on an 18-month contract, at this away ground. In 2020-21, after Erol Bulut's departure, he managed 10 games: 7 wins, 2 draws, 1 defeat. His last game was on 15 May 2021, away at Kayseri. AA puts the gap at 2,001 days, roughly five and a half years.[1] From Kartal to Kuyt's interim spell, see that column; for the Liverpool night, our 3-2 full-time column; for the derby, our Galatasaray 2-1 column.

Burak Yılmaz says neither the 3-2 over Liverpool nor the long wait for a title changes how big Fenerbahçe are. He adds that they will play their own game: "Not with hesitation; God willing, we want to play good football and take points at the end." "We can see our tickets have sold out," he says. The threshold line is his too: "Maybe this is a threshold game for us."[4]

This is my reading: the threshold is built on the only home league win under Burak. A draw on 16 points does not move goal difference; the overtaking AA writes about can only come with three points. A Fenerbahçe defence that has conceded 10 and a Çorum defence that has conceded 16 are two ends of the same points total. I read Sunday evening as a one-goal away win for Fenerbahçe.
