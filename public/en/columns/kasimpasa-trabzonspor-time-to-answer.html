What Begins at Kasımpaşa Is More Than a Season: It Is Trabzonspor's Time to Answer

FutbolRadar 15 August 2026

Trendyol Süper Lig 2026-27, Matchday 1 | Kasımpaşa vs Trabzonspor | Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Stadium, 15 August 2026, 19:00

A matchday morning in Trabzon is not like other mornings. The talk at the fish market changes, the starting eleven is debated in the barber's chair, the presenter's voice on the minibus radio is turned up a notch. The sea is the same sea, the mountain the same mountain; but the city's pulse is locked onto one hour of the evening. Today that hour is 19:00.

The match is in Istanbul. The Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Stadium, matchday one of the Trendyol Süper Lig's Adnan Süvari Season, Kasımpaşa the opponent, Gürcan Hasova with the whistle, beIN Sports 1 with the broadcast. On paper, an ordinary opening fixture. But there is no ordinary match on the back of the burgundy-and-blue shirt; only matches whose meaning has not been written yet.

Why there is more than three points tonight

Trabzonspor finished last season third in the league with the tenth Turkish Cup in the club's history in their hands. In the same season they lost the Super Cup final 4-1 to Galatasaray. Those three sentences describe the same team, and that is exactly why tonight matters: Trabzonspor have to say which sentence they are the continuation of.

On top of that came the weight of the summer. Mohamed Salah is in this squad now. There was a signing ceremony at Papara Park, the club broke its season-ticket record, and the English press wrote about Trabzon under headlines about "a Black Sea answer to Istanbul's hegemony". All of it is lovely. None of it is the pitch.

Expectation is not a privilege, it is an invoice. The payment schedule starts tonight.

The right frame of mind

The classic trap of opening-weekend football is caution. The rhythm has not settled, everyone fears the first defeat, teams spend twenty minutes feeling each other out. That is exactly what Trabzonspor must not do.

In their Austrian camp the burgundy-and-blues lost to Eintracht Frankfurt and Al Sadd, then closed the trip by beating Udinese 1-0. So pre-season was neither a collapse nor a festival — it was ordinary. Teams coming out of an ordinary pre-season can only make a difference on opening day through their intent.

Trabzonspor should come out tonight to build the result, not to wait for it. A team that keeps the ball in the opponent's half in the first quarter of an hour, gets to second balls a step earlier and does not delay the press trigger can settle this match without stretching it to ninety minutes.

The absentees have changed the plan

This is tonight's most concrete problem. Three names are missing from the squad list the club released: Tim Jabol-Folcarelli had meniscus surgery after picking up an injury in a friendly, Ernest Muçi is injured, and Arseniy Batagov was left out.

Those three absences hit three different areas. Folcarelli was the midfield's volume of combat. Muçi produced the best moment of the camp, the fourth-minute goal against Udinese. And Batagov's absence closes off the centre-back rotation; the Savic-Nwaiwu pairing is not a choice tonight, it is a necessity.

The shape being trailed in the press is this: André Onana in goal, Pina, Nwaiwu, Savic and Cabral in defence, Benjamin Bouchouari and Ruslan Malinovskyi in the middle, Paul Onuachu up front. Ozan Tufan starting on the bench, Aral Şimşir used in a more central role, and Metehan Mimaroğlu and Noah Saviolo considered out wide are all possibilities. None of it is settled; it is an equation Tekke will work on until the last training session.

The answer to the equation is clear: it will not be enough for Bouchouari to distribute the ball tonight, he has to win half the second balls. Malinovskyi has the squad's most expensive weapon — passing range and shooting quality from outside the box. Against an opponent sitting deep, the detail that unlocks the door will most likely come from his foot.

Responsibility in attack

Paul Onuachu's job is not only to finish. A centre-forward who keeps both centre-backs permanently occupied and can lay second balls off to a team-mate creates space directly for the wide players and for Aral Şimşir. Onuachu's real task tonight is to open the pitch up.

Aral Şimşir is the team's rhythm setting. Whether he looks forward or sideways with his first touch changes Trabzonspor's attacking speed completely. Count how many times he looks forward in the first half-hour — you can read the shape of the match from there.

As for the Salah question: the Egyptian is in the squad, but he is not expected to start given his lack of training time. That needs reading properly — Salah is not tonight's saviour, he is its breaking point. In a match still goalless on 60 minutes, him simply starting to warm up on the touchline pushes the Kasımpaşa defence backwards. Even that is a gain.

Underestimate the opponent and you come back from Istanbul empty-handed

Emre Belözoğlu remains in charge of Kasımpaşa from last season. The first half-hour is always high for a home side playing on opening night in front of its own supporters. Trabzonspor arrive at the 40th meeting between the two clubs with the overall edge; of 34 league matches played, the burgundy-and-blues have won 16, Kasımpaşa 8, with 10 drawn. They came away from this same ground with a 1-0 win last season. But statistics rescue nobody after the first whistle.

Kasımpaşa's most genuine threat will be the moments Trabzonspor lose the ball. A team taking the field two players short in the middle is the most exposed of all to the counter-attack. The spaces the burgundy-and-blues can use are just as clear: width and depth on the flanks, Onuachu's aerial dominance, and the pressure Malinovskyi can generate from set pieces.

The key match-up: the forward runs of Cabral and Pina against the recovery discipline of Kasımpaşa's wide players. The winner of that duel will determine which half of the pitch this match is played in.

The first goal writes everything

In the season's opening match, the first goal is more than the scoreline: it hands out courage. If Trabzonspor find it early, Kasımpaşa have to open up — and an opponent that opens up is this team's favourite kind. The reverse holds too: a side that goes behind before its rhythm has settled can get stuck in front of the penalty area under opening-night nerves.

Which is why the first 25 minutes matter more than the remaining 65.

And the stands

There are people who paid three thousand lira for the away end and travelled to Istanbul. There are supporters who broke the season-ticket record. Trabzonspor's away support is not a group of spectators, it is an argument; it changes the acoustics of a stadium and buys its own players half a second.

Only one thing is being asked of you: do not go quiet in a bad opening twenty minutes. The moment this team needs the stands most is the moment things are not going well.

Last word

Nobody knows what the score will be tonight. But how it is played does need to be known: a Trabzonspor that is aggressive from the first whistle, does not duck a duel, tries to win the ball back within six seconds of losing it, and does not shrink when it goes behind.

A league table does not tell you about a season. Character does.

The trip to Kasımpaşa is not a beginning, it is a statement. And this city always makes its statements in the same place: on the pitch.
