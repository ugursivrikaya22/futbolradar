Rematch for Zero Points: Belgium in İzmir and the Maths of Staying in League A

FutbolRadar 10 November 2026

In their first season in League A, Türkiye are bottom of the group. The Belgium rematch is not a night of revenge; it is the threshold of last place and the direct-relegation maths.

A fourth place that ends on zero points sits closest to the two teams that go down directly this season.

In their first season in UEFA Nations League A, Türkiye are bottom of their group. Four games, four defeats, zero points. Two goals scored, eleven conceded. [1] On Thursday 12 November 2026, at 20:00 Turkish time, the rematch for that table will be played at İzmir's Gürsel Aksel Stadium: Türkiye v Belgium, game 5 in League A Group 1. It is on ATV, live and free-to-air. [3] The head coach is Sergen Yalçın.

The national team page and the Nations League table point to the same spot: last place.

Everyone has a place in the table

When matchday four was over, the group was clear. France top on 10 points, Italy second on 7, Belgium third on 6, Türkiye bottom on 0. [1] The goal columns are in the same table: France 7-2, Italy 8-5, Belgium 6-5, Türkiye 2-11. That day France beat Belgium 4-1 at home to stay top. [1]

Türkiye's four results stand one by one. 0-1 to France in Kocaeli, 1-4 to Italy in Bursa, 3-0 to Belgium away, 3-1 to Italy in Bologna. The goal in Bologna was Aral Şimşir's: it made it 1-1 in the 61st minute, Italy went back in front in the 64th and the game finished 3-1. [1]

Two wins would take Türkiye to 6 points. In today's table that would only draw level with Belgium. Italy are on 7, France on 10. In the 2026/27 format, the door to the quarter-finals is open to the group winners and runners-up. [4] On current points, that door is out of reach of these two games. The real maths lies elsewhere.

Finishing bottom does not mean automatic relegation this season

UEFA's explanation for 2026/27 draws a finer line than the previous season. In League A, group winners and runners-up go to the quarter-finals. The best two third-placed teams stay in League A. The two worst third-placed teams and the two best fourth-placed teams go into the A/B play-offs against League B runners-up. The teams that drop straight into League B are the bottom two of the four group fourths. [4]

Finishing bottom of the group is not, on its own, enough to say "League A is over". Where it could be enough is clear: a fourth place that ends on zero or very few points falls into that "bottom two" calculation. Ranking the four group fourths today is not this column's job. What we know is that Türkiye are fourth in their own group, without a point.

That is why Thursday night should be read not as a night of revenge, but as a threshold. A win puts 3 points on the board and breaks the zero at the bottom. A draw puts 1 point on the board; it does not end fourth place by itself. A defeat means still zero points with two games left. On Sunday it is France in Bordeaux: 15 November 2026, 22:45 Turkish time, Stade Atlantique. [2] Thursday's score changes what that away trip means. A Thursday without points leaves Sunday as the last line.

A rematch for the 3-0, not the same game

Belgium beat Türkiye 3-0 on their own ground. That score is closed. The night in İzmir is a different game: the same opponents, the reverse venue, a different kind of calendar pressure. Reducing the difference to simply "being at home" would be too easy. In this group Türkiye have lost at home too. France won 1-0 in Kocaeli, Italy 4-1 in Bursa. Gürsel Aksel is a new venue.

Belgium's group has not been smooth either. Two wins and two defeats in four games, six points. They lost 4-1 to France. [1] The team ahead of Türkiye is not unbeatable. The rematch is not there to paper over that night, but to answer the question it asked.

What needs fixing: eleven goals next to two

Conceding eleven goals in four games means 2.75 a game. Scoring two means one every two games. When those two numbers sit side by side, the problem cannot be written off as "a little more luck". We have already discussed, in a separate column, how these numbers show the defence is no footnote. The change since that column is this: the head coach is now Sergen Yalçın. The squad, the absentees and the starting XI are not in this column. Inventing an unannounced line-up would be describing a guess, not the game.

First, the rate of conceding. Eleven goals spread over four games. In Bologna, Şimşir's goal made it 1-1; soon afterwards Italy went back in front and the game finished 3-1. [1] If the side that equalises cannot hold the equaliser, points do not build up in League A either. The first thing to watch on Thursday night is not whether Türkiye go ahead. It is whether a second goal follows straight after the first one conceded.

Second, the shortage of goals. One of the two goals was Şimşir's. The other results were 0-1, 1-4 and 3-0. A night with one goal scored does not change the table when the opponents find three or four. Against Belgium the need is bigger than "one more goal". A team that has produced 2 goals against 11 conceded cannot be carried by a plan that hangs the game on a single shot.

Third, the state of the game. All four games ended in defeat. Türkiye have yet to win a game in this group, or even draw one. The team chasing and the team trying to hold a score do not play the same 90 minutes. In İzmir, the half-time score will shape the character of the second half.

Thursday and Sunday, two halves of the same sentence

Free-to-air coverage on ATV takes this game beyond the stadium too. [3] The maths on the screen is simple. A win puts 3 points on the board. Belgium are on 6 today; Türkiye would move to 3 and stay behind that total. Belgium's remaining fixtures beyond Thursday are not for this column to predict. A draw puts 1 point on the board: the zero is gone, but fourth place does not end by itself. A defeat carries zero points into a fifth game.

On Sunday at 22:45 France await in Bordeaux. France are on 10 points: 3 wins, 1 draw, no defeats. [1] Türkiye's only test against France this season ended 0-1 in Kocaeli. The second test, away from home, will be played with whatever is left over from Thursday night. If that is 3 points, Bordeaux stops being a rescue mission. If it is 0 or 1 point, Bordeaux is the last line.

FutbolRadar's reading

This column is not a score prediction. What we are building is narrower: Türkiye opened their first League A season at the bottom, without a point and with a 2-11 goal record. The Belgium rematch is the first home night on which that opening can be corrected. The correction will be measured not by a narrative but by the rate of goals conceded and the number of goals scored.

UEFA's rule this season does not relegate every group's bottom team automatically. That is not a sentence of relief. A fourth place that ends on zero points sits closest to the two teams that go down directly this season. Points not taken in İzmir will not come back by themselves on Sunday night in Bordeaux.
