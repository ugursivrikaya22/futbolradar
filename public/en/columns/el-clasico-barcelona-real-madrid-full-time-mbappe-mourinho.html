Mbappé's Night at Camp Nou: Real Madrid Win El Clásico 2-1, Mourinho on His Knees

FutbolRadar 26 October 2026

Barcelona's nine-from-nine run ended in El Clásico; Mbappé's two goals and Mourinho's disciplined Madrid cut the gap to 3 points.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

The Perfect Run Is Broken at Camp Nou

Sunday 25 October 2026. There were 62,055 people at the Spotify Camp Nou. All of them were waiting for the same sentence: ten wins from ten.

Barcelona had arrived with nine from nine in the league. A win would have been a first in La Liga history [2]. But El Clásico does not read the history books; it writes them.

When referee Juan Martínez Munuera blew the final whistle, the scoreboard was clear: Barcelona 1, Real Madrid 2 [1][3][4]. Our pre-match analysis: El Clásico preview · The rivalry page: El Clásico.

Before the Game: The Numbers Pointed to Barcelona

On paper the scales tipped towards the Catalans. Opta's pre-match prediction gave Barcelona a 54.4% chance of winning.

According to FutbolRadar's pre-match data, Raphinha led the scoring race with 12 league goals. Lamine Yamal had scored 8 in 9 games. On the other side was an Mbappé arriving with 12 goals in 12 games.

Real Madrid were without the suspended Vinícius. There was a last-minute surprise in goal too: Lunin came in for Courtois, who had the flu [6].

And the most electric line of the story: José Mourinho was returning to El Clásico on the Real Madrid bench.

First Half: Madrid Waited, Then Struck

Barcelona wanted the ball. Real Madrid chose to stay solid at the back and break quickly.

Arda Güler knocked on the door twice in the early stages. One shot went narrowly wide, the other was saved by Joan García.

41st minute. A fine collective Madrid attack ended with a cross from Yan Diomande. Gerard Martín's attempted clearance hit Mbappé and went in. A lucky goal, but there was a flawless passing move behind it. 0-1.

The half ended with that score. Camp Nou fell silent for the first time.

The Controversial Moment: Cancelo and Diomande

56th-57th minutes. João Cancelo stepping on Diomande with his studs became the most talked-about moment of the game.

Real Madrid wanted a red card. The referee did not show a card, and no punishment followed afterwards either. Diomande was out of the game for a short time.

The Captain's Answer: Raphinha

Five minutes later Camp Nou was on its feet again.

61st minute. From a quickly taken corner, Pedri surprised everyone by laying the ball off to Raphinha. The Brazilian captain found the far post with a sudden strike. Lunin got a touch but could not stop it. 1-1 [4][5].

After that goal the game changed. Barcelona turned up the pressure, and Madrid retreated into their own half.

Lunin's Hand, Yamal's Foot

66th minute. Lunin kept out Gerard Martín's powerful header from a corner with a magnificent dive. It was a save that meant nobody missed Courtois.

78th minute. Lamine Yamal beat two players and sent the ball towards goal. It went just past the post. Half of Camp Nou thought it was a goal.

Barcelona's second goal was at the door. But football sometimes punishes you just when you are playing your best.

87th Minute: Bellingham's Turn, Mbappé's Signature

Jude Bellingham beat his man with a roulette and gave the ball to Arda Güler. Güler spotted Camavinga's run.

Eduardo Camavinga left Pau Cubarsí behind with a single touch and cut the ball back. Mbappé put it into the empty net. 1-2 [3][4].

According to Real Madrid's official website, this was Mbappé's 100th goal in a Real Madrid shirt [3].

Mourinho slid on his knees. The bench spilled onto the pitch. This time, only Madrid's voice could be heard at Camp Nou.

Flick's Question Went Unanswered

Before the game Barcelona's biggest question mark was the defence. Tonight both goals again came from moments at the back: one from an unfortunate clearance, the other from a transition.

It was a bad week for Hansi Flick's side. After losing to PSG in the Champions League, Barcelona were beaten by Madrid too.

They are still top. But the gap is now 2 points to Atlético Madrid and 3 to Real Madrid [6]. Current table: La Liga standings.

Mourinho's Night

According to Sky Sports, this was Real Madrid's first win in Barcelona in three years [5].

This was a Madrid with less of the ball but more edge. Patient, disciplined and ruthless. A true Mourinho team.

Final Word: El Clásico Wrote Its Own Story Again

The numbers said Barcelona. The run said Barcelona. Camp Nou said Barcelona.

But Mbappé wrote the last sentence of the night. And El Clásico reminded us once again that even the best script always has a twist.
