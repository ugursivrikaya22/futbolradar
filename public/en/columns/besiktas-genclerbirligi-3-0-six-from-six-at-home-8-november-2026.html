Six From Six in the Eagle's Nest: Dolmabahçe Heals the Celtic Wound

FutbolRadar 08 November 2026

Vlahovic scored in the 20th and 45th minutes, Poku in the 85th; Trossard assisted all three.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

From the Glasgow cold to the Dolmabahçe warmth

On Thursday night at Celtic Park our hearts were tested once again. The hope that came with Orkun's lovely late strike to make it 2-2 froze when Camilo Duran scored in the 87th minute. The first defeat in Europe came on top of the league draw at Kasımpaşa. [3]

Three days later a very different story was written at Dolmabahçe. The Black Eagle healed its wound in its own nest, in front of its own supporters. Beşiktaş beat Gençlerbirliği 3-0 and go into the international break with their heads held high and smiles on their faces.

There were three goals on the scoreboard tonight. But what was felt in the stands, in living rooms and in cafés was much bigger: a fallen team getting back on its feet.

Vlahovic struck, Trossard wrote it, Poku signed it

The first act opened in the 20th minute. Dusan Vlahovic met Trossard's pass in behind the defence and drove it low, and Dolmabahçe rose: 1-0. [1]

Two minutes later came the turning point. Metehan Baltacı, trying to stop Vlahovic, was shown a second yellow card and Gençlerbirliği were down to ten men. This time the Eagle did not let the chance slip.

In the final minute of the first half the same partnership spoke again. Trossard broke on the counter from Murillo's pass, drew the goalkeeper and laid the ball off to Vlahovic; the Serbian striker did not miss the empty net: 2-0. That took Vlahovic to nine league goals this season. [1]

The final word came from the bench. Ernest Poku, on in the 77th minute, touched in Trossard's lofted ball on the line in the 85th to make it 3-0. Trossard had a hand in all three goals, and one word is enough for him: conductor. [2]

At the other end, the score could have been much heavier had İrfan Can Eğribayat not kept out shots from Orkun and Vlahovic. But tonight there was no wall that could stand in Beşiktaş's way.

The jinx is over, the fortress still stands: six from six at Dolmabahçe

This win also had a revenge side to it. Beşiktaş had lost their last two league games against Gençlerbirliği at Dolmabahçe. Last season's painful 2-1 was still fresh in the memory; tonight that chapter closed. [2]

And home became a fortress again. Beşiktaş have won all six of their home Süper Lig games this season. Vincenzo Italiano became the first coach in the club's history since Ljubisa Spajic in 1962-63 to win each of his first six home league games. [2]

Before the match Italiano recalled that they had not gone into the previous international break the way they wanted, and said that this time he wanted to go into it with a win and in a calmer mood. He hoped to leave the stadium happy at the end of the evening. The stands gave that wish the best possible answer. [5]

Reports that Italiano had come down hard on individual errors in defence were in the news on matchday. A night on which Nübel kept a clean sheet showed that the message had reached the pitch. [6]

Let us celebrate, but keep our feet on the ground

Still, our feelings should not run ahead of our heads. The opponents played close to 70 minutes with ten men, and Gençlerbirliği are 14th in the league. The real test is beating the late fragility we saw at Celtic in the big games too.

The table is clear as well. We are fourth on 22 points; leaders Galatasaray have 28, Trabzonspor 25 and Amed 23. We are six points off the top and three points ahead of our eternal rivals Fenerbahçe. [1]

After the break, captain Orkun Kökçü is suspended for the Konyaspor game because of the yellow card he picked up in the 55th minute. In Europe we host Hapoel Beer-Sheva on 26 November; to add to our seven points, Dolmabahçe has to be a fortress again that night. [1] [4]

The Eagle's wings, our hearts

Beşiktaş supporters do not love the score alone; they love their team's heart, the way it gets up from where it fell. The team that went down in Glasgow on Thursday night stood tall at Dolmabahçe on Sunday evening. That is exactly what makes us who we are.

Now it is time to breathe for two weeks. With Vlahovic's goals, every pass from Trossard's boot, Poku's young energy and the never-silent voice of the stands, the Black Eagle has spread its wings again.

The road is long and the climbs are steep. But tonight we saw it once more: whoever wears this black-and-white crest may fall, but gets back up. Bound from the heart, Beşiktaş until the end!
