Osimhen's Epic at Rams Park: Galatasaray Topple Fenerbahçe 2-1 in the Derby

FutbolRadar 26 October 2026

Osimhen's two first-half goals decided the derby; İrfan Can gave Fenerbahçe hope in the second half, but Galatasaray went top on 22 points.

This is an opinion column. It reflects the writer's personal view and should not be confused with FutbolRadar's verified match data.

Translated from the Turkish original: Read the original column in Turkish

One Night, One City, Two Colours

Monday 26 October 2026. 21:30. When the whistle blew at Rams Park, half of Istanbul held its breath and the other half prayed.

Matchweek 9 of the Süper Lig, but nobody called it "just a matchweek". This was Galatasaray against Fenerbahçe. This was the derby.

When referee Atilla Karaoğlan blew the final whistle, the scoreboard recorded an unchangeable truth: Galatasaray 2, Fenerbahçe 1 [1][2][3]. Line-ups and match details are on our match page.

Osimhen: The King Takes the Stage

Some players play a game. Others become the game itself. Tonight Victor Osimhen was the latter.

29th minute. From Barış Alper Yılmaz's assist, Victor Osimhen rippled the net. Rams Park exploded. 1-0 [1][2].

Before Fenerbahçe could regroup, minutes before half-time, it was him again. 44th minute. This time the pass came from Davinson Sánchez, and once again Osimhen showed no mercy. 2-0.

Two goals in the first half of a derby... That is the stuff of stories. Tonight Osimhen wrote that story himself.

Half-Time: A Yellow-and-Red Dream

Going into the dressing room 2-0 up, Galatasaray were superior in every area of the game. They had the ball, applied pressure and pinned their opponents in their own half.

Fenerbahçe could barely breathe in the first half. The cards were flying: İrfan Can Kahveci was booked in the 10th minute, Milan Škriniar in the 11th, N'Golo Kanté in the 31st.

The tension hit the roof. But Galatasaray kept their cool.

Fenerbahçe's Answer: İrfan Can Kahveci

But a derby is not over until it is over. Fenerbahçe came out for the second half with a completely different spirit.

İsmail Kartal made his moves early [4]. Kerem Aktürkoğlu came on in the 55th minute and Kojo Peprah Oppong in the 56th.

And in the 62nd minute came the moment the yellow-and-navy fans had been waiting for. From Oğuz Aydın's pass, İrfan Can Kahveci sent the ball into the net. 2-1 [1][3].

Suddenly the game was alight again. A nervousness crept into the roar of Rams Park. Fenerbahçe were shouting "it's not over".

The Last Half-Hour: A War of Nerves

Okan Buruk did not stand idle on the touchline either. In the 64th minute he sent on Rafael Leão. In the 80th, three changes at once: Leroy Sané, Gabriel Sara and Eren Elmalı.

Fenerbahçe brought on Dorgeles Nene and Mert Hakan Yandaş in the 78th minute and Vedat Muriqi in the 83rd. Kartal threw everything at it.

In the 88th minute the hero of the epic, Osimhen, made way for Wilfried Singo, and the stands gave him a standing ovation.

The final minutes were a war of nerves. Rafael Leão was booked in the 89th minute and Archie Brown at 90+1. But the score did not change. When the final whistle blew, Rams Park roared once more.

Tactical Summary: Galatasaray in Command

Both teams lined up in a 4-2-3-1. But the side that set the rhythm of the game was, without question, Galatasaray.

Okan Buruk's players controlled the game with 61.7% possession. 23 shots, 8 of them on target. Fenerbahçe managed only 4 shots, 3 of which found the target.

Lucas Torreira and Mario Lemina in midfield did not let Fenerbahçe build play. Up front, Barış Alper Yılmaz, Yunus Akgün and Batrakov fed Osimhen.

In the Fenerbahçe goal, Ederson made 6 saves to stop the gap widening. He was the biggest reason the yellow-and-navies stayed on their feet tonight.

Fenerbahçe's 18 fouls and 5 yellow cards also showed how much they struggled. Galatasaray committed only 10 fouls and managed the game calmly with 86.2% pass accuracy.

An Earthquake in the Table

With this win Galatasaray went top on 22 points [5][6]. Fenerbahçe stayed on 16; the gap between the two eternal rivals grew to 6 points. Current table: Süper Lig standings.

Derbies are not just three points. But tonight those three points were the key to the summit.

Derbies in the Memory

The recent history of this rivalry has been fiery too. On 1 December 2025 Fenerbahçe and Galatasaray drew 1-1.

In the Super Cup on 10 January 2026, Fenerbahçe won 2-0. And on 26 April 2026 Galatasaray answered with a 3-0 win at home.

And now a new page: another yellow-and-red night at Rams Park [7][8]. Club pages: Galatasaray · Fenerbahçe.

Final Word: The Epic Has Been Written

On one side, a victory written by Osimhen's two goals. On the other, a Fenerbahçe who resisted to the end through İrfan Can's goal.

Galatasaray were the winners. But this derby was an unforgettable night for both sides.

Rams Park did not watch a game tonight. It witnessed an epic.
