Overview

Barcelona was founded on 29 November 1899 as Foot-Ball Club Barcelona and plays at the Camp Nou. It holds the Copa del Rey record with 32 titles and has won 29 La Liga titles and 5 Champions Leagues.

History

The club won its first La Liga title in 1929 and its first European Cup in 1991-92. It enjoyed a golden era under Pep Guardiola (2008–2012). Its latest Champions League came in 2014-15 and its latest La Liga title in 2025-26.
