Trabzonspor's Game Plan

Kasımpaşa – Trabzonspor · A shape built around four absentees, service to Onuachu and the question of when Salah appears.

What pre-season says and does not say

Trabzonspor played their pre-season friendlies at a camp in Austria; by the reported record they won one of four. They beat Udinese 1-0 on 2 August and lost 2-1 to Göztepe on 8 August.

Friendly results are not an indicator of league form: rotation, training load and the purpose of the match all sit outside the scoreline. No judgement about form is built on them here.

What they may indicate is simply this: the team does not enter the season at its most fluent, and the opening weekend is the first real sample of how that looks on the pitch.

An attack built around Onuachu

Onuachu is the reference point in Trabzonspor's front line. Crosses from wide, second balls and aerial duels in the box naturally gather around him.

For that to be productive, the wide players must look for depth as well as delivery. When Saviolo and Aral Şimşir run in behind, the opposing line drops and the space in front of Onuachu grows.

Malinovskyi's choice of pass between those two movements will decide whether the attack becomes predictable.

Salah's introduction is a plan in itself

Mohamed Salah being in the squad opens a separate heading for the second half. If he is expected to start on the bench, when he comes on becomes a decision tied to the scoreline.

With Trabzonspor ahead, his introduction is a game-management move; level or behind, it is an attempt to open the match up. The same player, two different jobs.

Everything in this section rests on reported expectation, not on a game plan announced by the club.
