A forced choice in goal

Fenerbahçe start this match with their second-choice goalkeeper: Ederson is suspended and Mert Günok was left out injured. Only two keepers travelled, and Tarık Çetin is expected to start. That means the communication in front of the defence will be less familiar than usual on the opening weekend — particularly at set pieces and when claiming crosses.

Levent Mercan at left-back

Jayden Oosterwolde's injury leaves a gap at left-back. Archie Brown is in the squad; the name highlighted in pre-match coverage is Levent Mercan. The two have different profiles, and that directly affects how high Fenerbahçe push on the left. Whoever starts, how the attacking workload on the left is shared remains one of the tactical questions of the night.

Talisca through the middle, Muriqi on the bench

Romelu Lukaku is not registered yet and Vedat Muriqi is on the bench. The reported XI puts Talisca at the tip. Talisca attacks the box differently from an orthodox number nine: he drops between the lines to receive and creates his own shooting distance. That choice can blur the reference point for Gençlerbirliği's centre-backs.

Lyon is three days away

Fenerbahçe host Lyon in the Champions League play-off on 18 August. That is a fact likely to shape the substitutions as much as the starting XI: if the scoreline settles early the case for resting players grows; if the match stays tight the same eleven stay on longer. Squad management may be the most visible subplot of the night.
