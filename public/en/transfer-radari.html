Premier League

Reported24 November 2026

Omar Berrada: stadium plans will limit transfer spending

In a Sky Sports interview on 24 November 2026, Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada said plans for a 100,000-seat stadium will limit transfer spending in the coming windows. He said the club might invest a bit more in the squad in the short term, but has to think about the long term too.

Source: Sky Sports

Claim23 November 2026

Claims on Manchester United in January

Sky Sports Paper Talk, citing the Daily Mail and The Times, reported claims that Nathan Collins is a January candidate for Manchester United ahead of Calvin Bassey and Jarrad Branthwaite; that Mauricio Pochettino would have less than £50 million, with only two signings expected (one of them possibly a loan); and that a striker is the priority. Alejandro Balde and Rayan Aït-Nouri are listed again as left-back names. The Daily Mail line also values Branthwaite at over £70 million.

Source: Sky Sports

La Liga

Claim24 November 2026

Claim around a possible Alejandro Balde sale

Marca wrote on 24 November 2026 that Barcelona would not rule out selling Alejandro Balde but would not let him leave for nothing, put the market figure at about €40 million, and said the club is waiting for Jorge Mendes to bring an offer that matches his quality. No bid is confirmed. Franc Artiga, the coach who brought Balde through the academy, said on Sport's La Posesión that selling him would be a mistake if money is set aside. Marca also wrote that Hansi Flick has Cancelo and Xavi Espart ahead of Balde at left-back.

Source: Marca

Reported24 November 2026

Luis Enrique: PSG will not buy in the winter
