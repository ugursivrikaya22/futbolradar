Date, kick-off and stadium

UEFA's 2026/27 league-phase fixture list places Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday 24 November 2026, matchday 5. Kick-offs are 21:00 CET unless a different time is printed, and this match has no separate time. The matchday 5 notes repeat the same rule.

21:00 CET is 23:00 in Türkiye (UTC+3). Arsenal's official preview says Emirates Stadium, 8pm UK. Borussia Dortmund's official travel bulletin says 20:00 local time and 21:00 German time at Emirates Stadium. BBC Sport also shows a 20:00 kick-off. Those clocks are the same moment: 23:00 Türkiye time.

Where to watch in Türkiye

TRT Spor's own schedule, published on 23 November 2026, lists Tuesday's matchday 5 channels. At 23:00, Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund is on Tabii Spor 1.

Referee

Arsenal.com names Maurizio Mariani, assistants Daniele Bindoni and Stefano Alassio, fourth official Andrea Colombo, VAR Marco Di Bello and assistant VAR Daniele Chiffi. Borussia Dortmund's travel bulletin names the same referee, the same assistants, Andrea Colombo and VAR Marco Di Bello.

A UEFA.com officials page for this specific match was not confirmed when this page was written. The appointment above comes from the two clubs' official previews.

Probable line-ups

Official starting line-ups have not been announced. The names below are UEFA's predicted line-ups from 24 November 2026, not a club team sheet.

Arsenal (UEFA prediction): Arrizabalaga; White, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Lewis-Skelly, Ødegaard; Madueke, Havertz, Tzolis. Out, according to UEFA: Raya (shoulder), Saka (muscle), Zubimendi (hamstring). Doubts: none.

Borussia Dortmund (UEFA prediction): Kobel; Gadou, Anton, Schlotterbeck; Ryerson, Veerman, Svensson, Nmecha; Bellingham, Fábio Silva; Beier. Out, according to UEFA: Inácio (meniscus), Kaba (shoulder). Doubts: Chukwuemeka (muscle), Guirassy (thigh).

Arsenal.com published a separate fitness note and did not present it as a starting eleven. David Raya misses the game with a shoulder injury. Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi stay out for the next few weeks. Max Dowman can play in every competition again after a domestic ban. For Dortmund, Serhou Guirassy and Carney Chukwuemeka trained on Monday. Waldemar Anton is expected to be available. Giannis Konstantelias, Emre Can and Samuele Inacio are out long-term with knee injuries. Loanee Ethan Nwaneri can play against his parent club. That note does not match UEFA's doubt list line for line. The two reports stay separate. This page does not merge them into a new eleven.
