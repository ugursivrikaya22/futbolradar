Cevad Prekazi remembered at Galatasaray's General Assembly, Mustafa Denizli became emotional

Galatasaray held its ordinary November General Assembly on 11 November 2026 at the Özhan Canaydın Conference Hall at RAMS Park. According to the club's official statement, Alp Yalman and Mustafa Denizli spoke in memory of Cevad Prekazi. Sporx reported that Denizli had difficulty speaking and struggled to hold back tears.

GalatasarayMustafa DenizliCevad PrekaziGeneral Assembly

Galatasaray Sports Club held its ordinary November General Assembly on Wednesday 11 November 2026 at the Özhan Canaydın Conference Hall of the Ali Sami Yen Sports Complex RAMS Park. According to the official statement published by the club the same day, former president Alp Yalman and Mustafa Denizli, who served Galatasaray as a player and as a manager, spoke in memory of the late former player Cevad Prekazi.

According to Sporx's report of 11 November 2026, Denizli had difficulty speaking and struggled to hold back tears. In the remarks carried by Sporx, Denizli recounted his last conversation with Prekazi: "He said, 'I am going to New Zealand to be with my brother, and I will also receive treatment there.' That was our last conversation." Sporx reported that Denizli ended his remarks with: "May God have mercy on him. Our condolences to all of us, and to the club."

In a statement published on 9 November 2026, Galatasaray announced that Cevad Prekazi had died at the age of 69. According to the club, Prekazi was born on 18 August 1957 in Mitrovica, Kosovo, and scored 40 goals in 185 appearances for Galatasaray between 1985 and 1991. Anadolu Agency also reported the death on 8 November 2026.
